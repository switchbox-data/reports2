## I. SUMMARY

Through this Order, the Commission approves Versant Power's rate design compliance filing.[^1]

## II. COMPLIANCE FILING

On September 27, 2022, the Commission approved a Stipulation in this proceeding regarding new and revised changes to Versant Power's (Versant) rates designed to promote state policies. _Order Approving Stipulation (Versant Power)_. Specifically, the rate changes are designed to support customer use of resources allowing beneficial electrification of transportation and heating, including electric vehicle charging, heat pump use, and energy storage battery charging.

On October 18, 2022, Versant submitted Tariff Sheets to comply with the approved Stipulation. On November 22, 2022, Versant submitted a corrected compliance filing containing Tariff Sheets for Electric Vehicles, Battery Storage, and Heating for the Versant Bangor Hydro District (BHD) and the Versant Maine Public District (MPD). The filing and attached Tariffs Sheets updated the October 18, 2022 filing and includes only the rates proposed to go into effect November 30, 2022.

Versant indicated that it will name and refer in educational materials to the new or revised rates as "Eco Rates" designed to help residents and businesses save money through efficient use of electrification technologies. Versant will conduct outreach to educate customers about the opportunity to avoid monthly metering costs or monthly minimum charges on the second residential account at the same service locations by identifying the optional separately metered rates as the "Home Eco Rate with Bonus Meter." Because utility metering requirements, minimum bills and metering expenses may be difficult to communicate clearly to the average electricity user, Versant states that a name clearly indicating that the rate is a second metered service with no monthly charges because the residential customer already pays monthly charges on their "whole

## NOTICE OF RIGHTS TO REVIEW OR APPEAL

5 M.R.S. § 9061 requires the Public Utilities Commission to give each party at the conclusion of an adjudicatory proceeding written notice of the party's rights to seek review of or to appeal the Commission's decision. The methods of review or appeal of Commission decisions at the conclusion of an adjudicatory proceeding are as follows:

1. Reconsideration of the Commission's Order may be requested under Section 11(D) of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (65-407 C.M.R. ch. 110) within 20 days of the date of the Order by filing a petition with the Commission stating the grounds upon which reconsideration is sought. Any petition not granted within 20 days from the date of filing is denied.

2. Appeal of a final decision of the Commission may be taken to the Law Court by filing, within 21 days of the date of the Order, a Notice of Appeal with the Administrative Director of the Commission, pursuant to 35-A M.R.S. § 1320(1)-(4) and the Maine Rules of Appellate Procedure.

3. Additional court review of constitutional issues or issues involving the justness or reasonableness of rates may be had by the filing of an appeal with the Law Court, pursuant to 35-A M.R.S. § 1320(5).

Pursuant to 5 M.R.S. § 8058 and 35-A M.R.S. § 1320(6), review of Commission Rules is subject to the jurisdiction of the Superior Court.

Note: The attachment of this Notice to a document does not indicate the Commission's view that the particular document may be subject to review or appeal. Similarly, the failure of the Commission to attach a copy of this Notice to a document does not indicate the Commission's view that the document is not subject to review or appeal.
