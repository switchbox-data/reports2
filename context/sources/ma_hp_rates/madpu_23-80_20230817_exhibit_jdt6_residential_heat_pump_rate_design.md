_Extractor note (not source text): Arithmetic checks on the printed values._

- _Fixed Cost Recovery in Volumetric Rate (column) equals Cost Recovery per Month minus $8.50 in every month, and equals RD-1 Average Monthly Use × $0.10585 (e.g. January 684.49 × 0.10585 = 72.45)._
- _Winter (Nov–Apr) Fixed Cost Recovery in Volumetric Rate = 72.45 + 67.16 + 58.49 + 46.78 + 45.28 + 58.27 = $348.43; the exhibit prints $348.44 on line 21 [source discrepancy: 1 cent, consistent with rounding of unrounded spreadsheet values]._
- _Winter (Nov–Apr) Avg. Monthly Use with Heat Pump = 2,137.04 + 2,019.85 + 1,632.76 + 1,130.16 + 864.76 + 1,434.63 = 9,219.20 kWh; 348.44 / 9,219.20 = 0.03779._
- _Annual heat pump use minus annual RD-1 use = 12,383.13 − 6,455.74 = 5,927.39 kWh. The exhibit does not print this difference; Unitil's response to DOER 2-02(c) (a different document) refers to an "estimate of additional kWh ... of 5,927"._
- _The winter distribution charge of $0.03779 is 35.7% of the RD-1 distribution charge of $0.10585 (0.03779 / 0.10585); this ratio is not printed on the exhibit._
- _The summer months (May–October) have the same Heat Pump kWh as RD-1 kWh in the table; heat pump use differs from RD-1 use only in November–April._
