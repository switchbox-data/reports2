**Request No. DPU-Common 2-3:**

Refer to the Companies' Joint Comments at 4-5. Please:

(a) explain whether and how the Company will customize marketing, education, and outreach ("MEO") messaging, communication channels, or calls to action to differentiate between customers with a heat pump rebate on record and those who do not; and

(b) discuss any barriers that may exist to developing targeted MEO strategies for these distinct customer segments.

**Response:**

Customers who receive a qualifying heat pump rebate through Mass Save will be auto-enrolled in the Heat Pump Rate. These customers will receive information on the rate throughout the rebate exploration and application process. A Mass Save branded informational leave-behind postcard was created for contractors to give to customers. This card informs customers that they will be auto-enrolled once the heat pump incentive has been processed and provides guidance on how to self-enroll in the rate for immediate enrollment. Once enrolled, customers will receive a letter from Unitil notifying them of the rate change. The letter emphasizes the benefits of the rate but also contains instructions on opting out for customers who wish to do so.

Customers who received a heat pump rebate prior to 1/1/2019, or who install a heat pump independent of Mass Save will need to self-enroll. Where possible, Unitil has identified these customers and conducted targeted outreach. For example, in October, Unitil utilized an automated phone message and email to communicate to customers who received a heat pump incentive through Mass CEC beginning in 2015 and are not yet in the rate to inform them that they may be eligible. This was possible because the data was available to identify and target these customers.

Moving forward, Unitil will incorporate the Heat Pump Rate into its customer communications calendar and will conduct outreach to MA residential electric customers regarding the benefits of the Heat Pump Rate using communication channels such as on-bill messaging, email, and targeted social media. Unitil will target this outreach to customers who are not currently enrolled in the Heat Pump Rate when possible.

Unitil will also continue to conduct targeted stakeholder outreach to energy efficiency vendors and other partners utilizing its informational handout.
