# Docket 26-05-10: JPV-3, Representative Home Load Profiles and Marginal Costs

Workbook exhibit filed with Velez Direct in the Eversource Connecticut rate case. Gives the average fossil fuel-heated home's hourly load before and after the heat pump, the hourly marginal transmission and distribution costs, and the hourly marginal cost calculation and annual bills as formulas. Read for the calculation behind the testimony's $10 incremental marginal T&D cost and representative-home bills.

## Sheets

- **hourly_before** (8761 rows x 2 columns): timestamp, kwh
- **hourly_after** (8761 rows x 2 columns): timestamp, kwh
- **monthly** (13 rows x 3 columns): month_label, kwh_before, kwh_after
- **marginal_cost** (8761 rows x 3 columns): timestamp, mc_bulk_tx, mc_dist_sub_tx
- **hourly_marginal_cost** (8761 rows x 16 columns): timestamp, kwh_before, kwh_after, mc_bulk_tx_per_kwh, mc_dist_sub_tx_per_kwh, bulk_tx_dollars_before, dist_sub_tx_dollars_before, total_mc_dollars_before, bulk_tx_dollars_after, dist_sub_tx_dollars_after, total_mc_dollars_after, Component, ...
- **bills** (25 rows x 21 columns): Monthly consumption, representative fossil-fuel home, Rate 1, pre-retrofit, Rate 1, post-retrofit, Rate 1 rates used by the formulas
