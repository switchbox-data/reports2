# Docket 26-71: Exhibits 1, 2, and 3, Heat Pump Transmission Rate Design and Bill Impacts (Revised)

Exhibits 1 to 3 of National Grid's compliance filing in D.P.U. 26-71 (filed June 15, 2026; this version July 6, 2026), answering the DPU's June 1, 2026 order in D.P.U. 25-08-A to add a winter transmission charge to heat pump rates. Ten sheets of live formulas derive the charge from gas therms converted to kWh, with R-1 and R-2 bill impacts. The current winter transmission charge design.
