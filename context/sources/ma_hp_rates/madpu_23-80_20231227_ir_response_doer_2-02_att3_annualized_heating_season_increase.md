# Docket 23-80: DOER 2-02 Attachment 3, Annualized Increase in Heating Season Electric Usage

Attachment 3 to Unitil's Dec 27, 2023 response to DOER 2-02(c), which asked for a comparison of actual heat pump customers' heating load with the rate design model; it summarizes Attachment 2 and was discussed at the Feb 5, 2024 hearing. Its summary tab compares the measured heating-season kWh increase (mean 3,471) with the 5,927 kWh design assumption. Keep it for how a gas-proxy rate was checked against measured usage.
