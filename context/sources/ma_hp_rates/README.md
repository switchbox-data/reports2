# ma_hp_rates

Massachusetts seasonal heat pump rates (Eversource/NSTAR D.P.U. 25-55, National Grid D.P.U. 23-150 and 26-71, Unitil D.P.U. 23-80 and 26-72, and the cross-utility investigation D.P.U. 25-08). The core question these files answer: **how did each utility design a heat pump rate and enroll customers when it does not know where its heat pump customers are?** Rate design workbooks, the testimony that explains them, discovery responses on how customers are identified, and the DPU orders approving the rates.

## Quick finder: which file answers which question

**"How did you design a heat pump rate without knowing who has a heat pump?"** The designs do not need a list of heat pump customers. Rates are designed on the _class_ (R-1/RD-1) and assume heat pump load by converting each home's gas heating therms to kWh at a COP; customers then opt in or are enrolled by rebate record.

- **Method (workbooks, with live formulas):** Unitil model `madpu_23-80_20231023_exh_unitil_jdt_rate_design_model_electric_public_dpu12-13.xlsx` (the template National Grid copied); National Grid `madpu_23-150_20250110_..._exh_hp_3_revised_rate_design_analysis_excel.xlsx` and `..._attachment_ng4_excel.xlsx` (gas therms to kWh at a temperature-dependent COP); Eversource `madpu_25-55_20250417_exh_es_hp_2_heat_pump_rate_design.xlsx` plus the DOER alternate runs; 2026 winter transmission charge `madpu_26-71_..._exhibits_1_2_3_...xlsx` (National Grid) and `madpu_26-72_..._schedule_cjg1_...xlsx` (Unitil).
- **Method (testimony that explains the workbook):** Unitil Taylor `..._jdt1_...` and Exhibit JDT-6 (winter volumetric charge set at 36% of RD-1); Eversource Chin `..._exh_es_hp_1_testimony_final.md`; National Grid `..._exh_hp_1_implementation.md` (2.8x winter usage multiplier, lifted from Unitil JDT-6 per `..._responses_to_dpu_questions_hp_rate.md`).
- **Revenue-shift rate design that needs no heat pump list:** National Grid `..._ng_rr_dpu_42_attachment_excel.xlsx` and `..._ng_ir_dpu_39_8_attachment_excel.xlsx` (bill-frequency data).
- **"We cannot identify all eligible customers" (the utility admissions):** Eversource DPU-2-4 and AG-1-1 (no analysis of actual heat pump usage); Unitil DPU 2-04 (attestation + annual review), DPU-Common 2-3 and 3-5 (cannot identify non-Mass Save heat pumps); National Grid DPU-Common 2-3 and 3-5, `..._ng_ir_li_ng_1_8_...md` ("does not track customers using electric resistance heat"); Unitil hearing transcript Tr. 199-216 (no peak-demand data for existing heat pump customers).
- **How enrollment works anyway:** Mass Save rebate recipients are auto-enrolled (Eversource: rebates after Jan 1, 2019, plus about 12,000 manual enrollments; see DPU-2-4); everyone else applies with proof or attests. Measured usage used to check the assumption: Unitil DOER 2-02 attachments (3,471 kWh measured vs 5,927 kWh assumed).
- **Orders:** Eversource D.P.U. 25-55 final order (winter Nov-Apr; summer rate set at the standard R-1/R-2 rate); National Grid D.P.U. 23-150 order excerpt (rejects "Electrification Pricing", orders a Unitil-style rate); Unitil D.P.U. 23-80 order excerpt; cross-utility order D.P.U. 25-08-A (already in this directory).
