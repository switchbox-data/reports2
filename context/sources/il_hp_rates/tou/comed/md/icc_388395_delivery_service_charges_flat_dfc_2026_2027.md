**Extraction notes**:

- _Minor typographical errors in the source have been corrected for readability. Substantive corrections are annotated inline._
- The "RECEIVED / Jan 30 2025 / ERM #25-010" stamp is omitted. Standard sheet footer ("Filed with the Illinois Commerce Commission on January 30, 2025. Filed pursuant to the Illinois Commerce Commission Order dated January 16, 2025, in Docket No. 24-0378. Date Effective: February 6, 2025. Issued by David R. Perez, EVP and COO, Post Office Box 805379, Chicago, Illinois 60680-5379") is stated once.
- **"& ADJ" means the value is a base charge before adjustment factors.** For the TOU DFCs, Info Sheet 67.1 (companion file) gives the billed charge as the base multiplied by (IDUF + DRAF + EDAF + TPAF + RBAFD), plus DGRAD. For the flat charges the adjustment components differ in detail (see the billed-basis table in `comed_guide_billed_delivery_service_charges_oct2026.md`). **The DFC values below are therefore not what appears on a bill.**
- Dollar signs are escaped as `\$`. Legend: CC = customer charge (\$/month); SMSC = standard metering service charge (\$/month); DFC = distribution facilities charge (\$/kWh); IEDT = Illinois Electricity Distribution Tax charge (\$/kWh).
- The ICC order and the revenue requirements behind these charges: the 2025–2027 delivery charges are set by the multi-year rate plan order of December 19, 2024 (Docket Nos. 22-0486/23-0055/24-0181 consol.) and re-spread by class under the Docket 24-0378 order of January 16, 2025 (see transmittal letter).
