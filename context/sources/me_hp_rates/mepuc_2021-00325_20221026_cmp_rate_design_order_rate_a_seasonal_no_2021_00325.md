STATE OF MAINE
PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION

Docket No. 2021-00325

October 26, 2022

MAINE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION
Investigation into Transmission and Distribution Utility Rate Design to Promote State Policies

ORDER APPROVING SEASONAL RATE

BARTLETT, Chair; DAVIS and SCULLY, Commissioners

## I. SUMMARY

Through this Order, the Commission approves Rate A-Seasonal as a pilot program for residential customers that have installed heat pumps at their service location.

## II. BACKGROUND

On October 11, 2022, Central Maine Power Company (CMP) filed for approval of proposed Rate A-Seasonal which would be available to customers verbally affirming heat pump ownership at their service location.

In its filing, CMP stated that, on September 27, 2022, the Commission issued its Order Approving Stipulation (Order) in this proceeding. As part of the Order, the Commission approved a Revised Stipulation dated September 22, 2022 that included revisions to several Electric Delivery Rate Schedules, namely Rate A-LM, Rate A-TOU-OPTS, and Rate B-DCFC.

In addition, Paragraph 9 of the Revised Stipulation provided that the Stipulating Parties agree that in addition to these rate design changes, CMP will continue to collaborate with the Stipulating Parties to develop an additional new pilot residential heat pump rate. The Commission indicated that the proceeding would remain open to allow resolution of the proposed pilot additional residential heat pump rate.

In its October 11th filing, CMP stated that it has worked with other Stipulating Parties to develop the proposed Rate A – Seasonal (attached to the filing), which would be available to customers verbally affirming heat pump ownership at their service location. This proposed rate has been circulated to the parties to this proceeding via electronic mail. CMP stated that it has received limited feedback, which feedback has been incorporated into the proposed Rate A –Seasonal. Furthermore, CMP stated that it has received no feedback adverse to the proposed rate.

On October 12, 2022, a procedural order was issued allowing parties to file comments in opposition to CMP's proposed rate. On October 19, 2022, the Industrial

<!-- PDF page 2 (printed page "-2-") -->

Energy Consumer Group filed comments in support of the proposed pilot rate. The Commission did not receive any filings in opposition to CMP's proposed rate.

## III. PROPOSED PILOT RATE

The proposal is for a pilot rate, which would be available to no more than 5,000 customers and would automatically terminate on or upon the earlier of October 31, 2024 or the adoption of a replacement residential beneficial electrification rate or rates by the Commission.

The proposed rate would have a monthly Service Fee of $31.67. The kWh charge from May through October would be $0.161867 per kWh, and the kWh charge for the remainder of the year would be $0.003478 per kWh. The rate was designed to be revenue neutral when compared to the revised Rate A-TOU-OPTS rate approved in this proceeding.

CMP notes that, although availability of this rate is predicated on a customer electing to take service under this rate on a year-round basis for a period of at least twelve months, a customer would be allowed to opt out of this rate before the required twelve-month period is complete, provided that any such customer would not be eligible to participate in this rate for a period of one year from the date on which service under this rate was terminated.

## III. DECISION

Based upon a review of CMP's October 11, 2022 filing and the lack of any opposition from the Stipulating Parties, the Commission approves Rate A-Seasonal as a pilot program for residential customers that have installed heat pumps at their service location.

Accordingly, it is

**ORDERED**

1. That Central Maine Power Company's proposed Rate A-Seasonal is, hereby, approved.

## NOTICE OF RIGHTS TO REVIEW OR APPEAL

5 M.R.S. § 9061 requires the Public Utilities Commission to give each party at the conclusion of an adjudicatory proceeding written notice of the party's rights to seek review of or to appeal the Commission's decision. The methods of review or appeal of Commission decisions at the conclusion of an adjudicatory proceeding are as follows:

1. Reconsideration of the Commission's Order may be requested under Section 11(D) of the Commission's Rules of Practice and Procedure (65-407 C.M.R. ch. 110) within 20 days of the date of the Order by filing a petition with the Commission stating the grounds upon which reconsideration is sought. Any petition not granted within 20 days from the date of filing is denied.

2. Appeal of a final decision of the Commission may be taken to the Law Court by filing, within 21 days of the date of the Order, a Notice of Appeal with the Administrative Director of the Commission, pursuant to 35-A M.R.S. § 1320(1)-(4) and the Maine Rules of Appellate Procedure.

3. Additional court review of constitutional issues or issues involving the justness or reasonableness of rates may be had by the filing of an appeal with the Law Court, pursuant to 35-A M.R.S. § 1320(5).

Pursuant to 5 M.R.S. § 8058 and 35-A M.R.S. § 1320(6), review of Commission Rules is subject to the jurisdiction of the Superior Court.

Note: The attachment of this Notice to a document does not indicate the Commission's view that the particular document may be subject to review or appeal. Similarly, the failure of the Commission to attach a copy of this Notice to a document does not indicate the Commission's view that the document is not subject to review or appeal.
