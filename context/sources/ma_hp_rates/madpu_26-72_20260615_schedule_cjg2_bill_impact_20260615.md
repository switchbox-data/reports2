# Docket 26-72: Schedule CJG-2 (Bill Impact)

Schedule CJG-2 of Unitil's June 15, 2026 compliance filing in D.P.U. 26-72, giving the bill impacts of the winter transmission charges built in Schedule CJG-1. The HP-RES and HP-RES LI tabs price a customer on the Exhibit Unitil-JDT-6 usage profile, beside a rate change summary and rate tabs. Keep it to show the 2026 bill impacts still rest on the 2023 gas-proxy usage profile.
