## ORDER

BY THE COMMISSION:

We adopt as our action the Recommended Decision of Deputy Chief Administrative Law Mark A. Hoyer dated October 1, 2024;

<!-- PDF page 2 (printed page 2) -->

THEREFORE,

IT IS ORDERED:

1. That the corrected Joint Petition for Approval of Settlement (Settlement) filed on August 16, 2024 by Duquesne Light Company, the Bureau of Investigation and Enforcement, the Office of Consumer Advocate, the Office of Small Business Advocate, the Coalition for Affordable Utility Services and Energy Efficiency in Pennsylvania, the Pennsylvania Weatherization Providers Task Force, Inc., and Walmart, Inc., be granted, and the Settlement be adopted, in full, without modification.

2. That Duquesne Light Company shall be permitted to file a revised tariff supplement in the form set forth in Appendix A to the corrected Joint Petition for Approval of Settlement, to become effective upon at least one day's notice, for service rendered on and after December 20, 2024, designed to produce an annual increase in revenues consistent with this Opinion and Order.

3. That Duquesne Light Company, the Bureau of Investigation and Enforcement, the Office of Consumer Advocate, the Office of Small Business Advocate, the Coalition for Affordable Utility Service and Energy Efficiency in Pennsylvania the Pennsylvania Weatherization Providers Task Force, Inc., and Walmart, Inc., shall comply with the terms of the corrected Joint Petition for Approval of Settlement as though each term and condition stated therein had been the subject of an individual ordering paragraph.

4. That the Formal Complaints of Ronald T. Bernick at Docket No. C-2024-3048340, David Anglero at Docket No. C-2024-3049302, and Robert Nesmith at Docket No. C-2024-3049624, in this proceeding be dismissed and marked as closed.

<!-- PDF page 3 (printed page 3) -->

5. That the Formal Complaints of the Office of Consumer Advocate at Docket No. C-2024-3047779 and the Office of Small Business Advocate at Docket No. C-2024-3048171 be deemed satisfied and marked closed.

6. That upon acceptance and approval by the Commission of the revised tariff supplement filed by Duquesne Light Company, this investigation proceeding shall be terminated and marked closed.

BY THE COMMISSION,

Rosemary Chiavetta
Secretary
