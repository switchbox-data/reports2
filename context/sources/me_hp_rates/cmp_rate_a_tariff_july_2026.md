# CMP Rate A Residential Service — Tariff Schedule

**Source:** [CMP Rate A Tariff (current)](https://www.cmpco.com/documents/d/cmp/a)
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2026
**Docket No.:** 2026-00011 and 2025-00139

## Basic Rate Per Month

- $27.37 for the first 50 kWh or less
- $0.119590 per kWh for all kWh in excess of above

\* Includes transmission charges in accordance with Subsection 44.1 of the Terms & Conditions and includes Efficiency Maine Trust assessment charges in accordance with Subsection 49.1 of the Terms & Conditions.

## Five Components of CMP Delivery Pricing

Per CMP's pricing page:

1. **Distribution** — local power lines, meter readers, bucket trucks
2. **Transmission** — large power lines and substations (FERC-regulated)
3. **Stranded Cost (Public Policy)** — renewable energy purchase power agreements, low-income assistance, energy efficiency programs
4. **Conservation Assessment** — funds energy efficiency initiatives (Efficiency Maine Trust)
5. **Electricity Lifeline Program (ELP)** — assistance to qualifying residential customers ($0.001468/kWh per Section 33.7)
