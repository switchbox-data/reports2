## CCANDR03-01

**Item No.: CCANDR03-01**

Please refer to Witness Karas’s rebuttal testimony at 22:1-17.

a) BGE’s website states that for customers enrolled in the Connected Rewards program, “Customers with a Heat Pump . . . are automatically enrolled in the year-round pathway.” How does BGE determine which customers have a heat pump and should therefore be automatically enrolled in the year-round pathway?

b) To the extent that entities separate from BGE administer the Connected Rewards program (e.g., smart thermostat providers), how do those entities determine which customers have a heat pump and should therefore be automatically enrolled in the year-round pathway?

c) Has the Commission reviewed the process that is used to determine which customers have a heat pump and should therefore be automatically enrolled in the year-round pathway?

i. If so, did the Commission approve the process?

**RESPONSE:**

a. BGE receives HVAC equipment configuration information as part of the customer enrollment data provided by the thermostat manufacturer associated with the device being enrolled, not because BGE independently verifies the existence of a heat pump. As of October 31, 2026, customers who have chosen to participate in Connected Rewards represent approximately 8% of BGE’s residential customers.

b. Information on customers with heat pumps is determined through HVAC equipment configuration provided by the participating thermostat manufacturer.

c. No. The Commission is not required to separately review or approve the administrative enrollment mechanics used to implement the Connected Rewards program. The Commission reviews and approves BGE's EmPOWER Maryland program offerings, including the applicable program designs, budgets, incentives, and demand response offerings. The process used to identify the HVAC equipment associated with an eligible smart thermostat and assign the customer to the appropriate participation pathway is an administrative implementation detail of the approved program and does not require separate Commission approval.
