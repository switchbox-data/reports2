# Ameren Illinois EV time-of-use sources (Docket P2026-0051)

Markdown extracts on Ameren Illinois's delivery-side EV programs: **ChargeSmart** (Rider EVCP: Preferred Charging Period credit, Non-Preferred Charging Period charge on the highest hour, first-12-months bill credit, DS-3/DS-4 off-peak demand charge elimination, rate limiter credit), **FlexCharge** (managed charging) and **ChargeReady** (make-ready incentives, new in surrebuttal). Context is the 2028-2031 Multi-Year Integrated Grid Plan (ICC Docket 26-0051; e-docket P2026-0051) and the beneficial electrification (BE) portfolio compliance dispute under 220 ILCS 5/16-105.17(f)(2)(M).

The original PDFs are **not stored** in the repo; `MANIFEST.csv` has size, pages, SHA-256 and source URL for each. ICC docket pages are CAPTCHA-gated for scripted downloads (the PDFs were pulled through a browser session).

## Known discrepancies and caveats

- **PCP hours.** The tariff (Rider EVCP) and Ex. 9.1 / 42.1 give the Preferred Charging Period as 11 P.M.-7 A.M.; WeaveGrid-related pages elsewhere in the docket refer to a 6 A.M. end. Use the tariff as authoritative.
- **Source arithmetic.** Ex. 9.1 prints a 2030 portfolio total of $5,454,272 where components sum to $5,454,273; Ex. 42.1 has similar $1-$9 rounding differences. Extracts keep the printed figures and flag the differences.
- **Not extracted** (optional, if needed): Cottrell exhibits (files 663581, 671742, 676614), the Proposed Order EV section (file 387084, pp. ~321-334), Staff and Ameren initial briefs (385432, 385438), and the BE Plan dockets that set Rider EVCP rates (22-0431/22-0443, 24-0494/24-0578, 25-0083). Hyperlink targets of label-only footnotes in the IEPA testimony are not captured.
