## D. Make Ready Incentive Provisions

Eligibility for the make-ready incentives will be determined through a detailed process with a third-party vendor, AIC engineering, and BE program teams. The descriptions and depiction are meant to serve as a high-level summary definition and visual representation. The actual qualifying costs will be determined through coordination with AIC.

The make-ready incentive is described as:

- **Standard Line Extension Policy (Utility Side)** – Covers the utility-owned electric infrastructure needed to connect and serve a new EV charger. This includes traditional distribution infrastructure required to reach the customer's point of delivery, including electric conductor, poles, and other appurtenances, as well as the installation of the utility transformer(s).
- **Supplemental Line Extension Allowance (Customer Side)** – Covers customer-owned electric infrastructure necessary to connect an EV charger. This infrastructure is downstream from the Point of Delivery and can include conductors, metering equipment, trenching for service lines, and labor costs. This excludes costs for the charging equipment and electric vehicle.

A depiction of the above descriptions is included below:

A green bracket labelled **"Utility Side Allowance"** spans the **utility service** (pole with overhead line) and the **utility transformer**. A blue bracket labelled **"Customer Side Allowance"** spans the **electric meter**, the **electric panel**, and the **wiring up to charger** (charger posts). A vertical line labelled **"Point of Delivery"** separates the two brackets, between the transformer and the meter, and a dotted line labelled **"SERVICE LINE"** runs from the utility service through the transformer and on to the meter, the panel and the charger.

_Incentive Detail_

[Ex. 42.1, page 10 of 10]

The make-ready incentive will be a maximum of $300/kW of connected EVSE. Facilities located in or charging electric vehicles that serve EIECs and/or LI communities will receive an additional $200/kW incentive, for a total of $500/kW of connected EVSE in kW.

For facilities located in or charges electric vehicles that serve EIECs and/or LI communities: Total allowance is calculated as a maximum amount of **$500 per connected kW**. |

If the customer's project cost for utility side make ready exceeds the make-ready incentive amount, customer will pay, in advance of construction, to the Company an amount equal to the difference between the actual cost and the make ready incentive.

If the customer's project cost for utility side make ready is less than the make-ready incentive amount, the customer will be eligible to apply any remaining incentive amount to the customer side make-ready infrastructure costs as outlined below.

**Eligibility**

Available for non-residential customers – including multifamily facilities, fleet facilities, public charging facilities, transit facilities and education facilities. These customers must have stand-alone service points that are primarily intended to provide power and energy to EVSE (non-EVSE will be equivalent to 10% or less of the connected kW load). For customers who take the make-ready incentive, additional provisions are laid out in Standards and Qualifications for Electric Service. Details on system expansion begin on page ten of the Standards and Qualifications for Electric Service document.
