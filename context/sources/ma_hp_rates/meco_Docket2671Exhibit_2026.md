**MASSACHUSETTS ELECTRIC COMPANY**
**NANTUCKET ELECTRIC COMPANY**
**TRANSMISSION SERVICE COST ADJUSTMENT PROVISION**

The Transmission Service Cost Adjustment shall collect from customers taking
transmission service under the Company’s rates the transmission costs billed to the Company by
New England Power Company (“NEP”), any other transmission provider, the New England
Power Pool, a regional transmission group, an independent system operator or any other entity
that is authorized to bill the Company directly for transmission services[^1]. The transmission
service cost adjustment shall be calculated separately for each of the Company’s rate classes and
shall be a cents per kilowatt-hour factor applicable to all kilowatt-hours delivered by the
Company to customers taking transmission service under each of the Company’s rates, with the
exception of those Rate G-3 customers opting into the Transmission Coincident Peak Billing
Option, whose rate will be a per kilowatt factor. The per kilowatt factor will be calculated based
on the transmission costs allocated to Rate G-3 customer rate class using the methodology
described below and divided by the most recently available analysis on the Rate G-3 demands.
Rate R-1 and Rate R-2 residential customers who were placed on the Heat Pump Rate pursuant
to the Rate R-1 and Rate R-2 retail delivery service provisions, subject to change from time to
time, shall be placed on a Heat Pump Transmission Service Charge, which will be calculated
using the methodology described below and divided by the forecasted heat pump customers
kilowatt hours for the winter season, which is defined as November 1 through April 30.

The factor shall be established annually based on a forecast of transmission costs, and
shall include a full reconciliation and adjustment for any over- or under-recoveries occurring
under the prior year’s adjustment. The Company shall allocate the forecast of transmission costs
and the balance of any over- or under-recoveries to its rate classes using an allocator based upon
the most recently available analysis which reports on the monthly rate class demands at the time
of NEP’s monthly transmission system peak. The Company may file to change the factor
adjustments at any time should significant over- or under-recoveries occur.

Any adjustment of the Transmission Service Cost Adjustment factors shall be in
accordance with a notice filed with the Department of Public Utilities (the “Department”) setting
forth the amount of the increase or decrease, and the new Transmission Service Cost Adjustment
amounts for each rate class. The notice shall further specify the effective date of such
adjustments, which shall not be earlier than thirty days after the filing of the notice, or such other
date as the Department may authorize.

The operation of this Transmission Service Cost Adjustment clause is subject to Chapter
164 of the General Laws.

[^1]: Hereinafter, “Transmission Provider” shall refer to these entities.
