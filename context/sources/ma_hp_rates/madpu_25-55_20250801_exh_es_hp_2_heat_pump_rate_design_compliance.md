# Docket 25-55: Heat Pump Rate Design (Exhibit ES-HP-2, Compliance)

Eversource's compliance version of Exhibit ES-HP-2, filed 8/1/25 with its compliance tariffs after the DPU's 7/29/25 order in D.P.U. 25-55. It reruns the original model with summer charges equal to R-1/R-2 and a November-April winter. We keep it as the source of the final approved prices and to compare against the original and the DOER-1-2 alternate runs, which shows what the DPU's changes did to the model.
