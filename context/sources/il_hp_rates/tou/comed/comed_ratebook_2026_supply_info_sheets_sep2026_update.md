**Reading guide (added by extractor, not source text)**: For a residential customer in **October 2026 through May 2027 nonsummer months**, the flat default supply is PEC 8.413 + PSC 1.690 = **10.103 ¢/kWh** plus the monthly PEA (October 2026: a 1.551 credit, so about 8.552). Rate BEST supply for the same months is the period BESTEC (4.656 / 14.700 / 6.009 / 3.394) + 2.039 + TPEA (October: 0.110). The delivery base charges are not in this excerpt. ComEd's October 2026 billed-charges guide (`comed_guide_billed_delivery_service_charges_oct2026.md`) shows the 2026 base charges equal to those in the January 2025 compliance filing (5.698 flat; 10.712 mid-day TOU for single-family without electric heat), with IDUFR 1.0217 and the other adjustment factors moving.
