# EV Registration Factsheet

This document serves to analyze the current demographic of the CT electric vehicle population to better inform public policy across the state. In collaboration with the CT DMV, DEEP receives vehicle registration data at the end of every six-month period. DEEP's EV Registration Factsheet is distributed following each period.

The EV Registration Factsheet includes an EV growth chart, EV categorizations, and an EV by Town index. Note: The following vehicle populations represent the total volume of EVs actively registered in the state on each specified date.

Revised: September 27, 2026
