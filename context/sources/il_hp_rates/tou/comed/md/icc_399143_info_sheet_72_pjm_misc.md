## 1st Revised Informational Sheet No. 72

(Canceling Original Informational Sheet No. 72)

**PJM SERVICES AND MISCELLANEOUS PROCUREMENT COMPONENTS CHARGE**

Supplement to Rate BEST (1)

| Period                                                                                                                                   | PJM Services and Miscellaneous Procurement Components Charge |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Applicable **prior to** the June 2026 monthly billing period (2) (3) (4)                                                                 | N/A                                                          |
| Applicable **beginning with the June 2026 monthly billing period and extending through the May 2027 monthly billing period** (2) (3) (4) | **1.875 ¢/kWh**                                              |

NOTES:

1. This informational sheet is supplemental to Rate BEST – Basic Electric Service Time-Of-Use Pricing (Rate BEST).
2. The PJM Services and Miscellaneous Procurement Components Charge is applied in the Supply Section on retail customer bills.
3. The PJM Services and Miscellaneous Procurement Components Charge includes the Residential Supply Base Uncollectible Cost Factor (SBUFR) listed in Informational Sheet No. 21.
4. The PJM Services and Miscellaneous Procurement Components Charge includes the Residential Incremental Supply Uncollectible Cost Factor (ISUFR) listed in Informational Sheet No. 20.

**Reading guide (added by extractor, not source text)**: This is a single flat ¢/kWh line (no season, no time-of-day variation) that applies **only to Rate BEST**. It covers PJM services and miscellaneous procurement costs. The closest flat-rate counterpart is the PSC on Info Sheet 2 (residential 1.722 ¢/kWh), but the sheet does not say how 1.875 maps onto the flat PEC and PSC components, so treat 1.875 as a Rate BEST-only line and compare bundled totals rather than line by line.
