# Docket 23-150: Exhibit NG-4, Residential Heat Pump Rate Design

Exhibit NG-4, enclosed with National Grid's January 22, 2025 letter in D.P.U. 23-150 answering the DPU's request to replicate Unitil's Heat Pump Rate Design tab (Exhibit Unitil-JDT-6, D.P.U. 23-80) with its own data. Six sheets with live formulas convert gas heating therms to kWh at a monthly COP to derive the 2.7x to 2.8x winter multiplier. Shows it rests on gas loads and a forecast, not measured heat pump customers.
