_Extractor note (not source text): Margin line numbers. Every exhibit page prints a column of row numbers down the left margin (1, 2, 3, ...); they are treated as line numbers (`citation_scheme: page_line`). On PDF pp. 21, 22 and 24 the margin numbers 1-9 are printed but the text-layer reader finds only 10 and above, so the page-header lines at rows 1-9 of those three pages (exhibit title, company, date, docket, page label) are transcribed without `{n}` markers; on PDF p. 23 all rows are detected and numbered. Tables keep the row number at the start of each table row, for example `| {12} Beginning Balance ...`. A `{13-15}` range marks a multi-row column heading. Rows with no printed text are left out._

_Extractor note (not source text): Fidelity. The text is verbatim from the PDF's text layer, checked against rendered page images. The text layer inserts stray spaces inside numbers (for example `1 ,651,486.7`, `$ 8 53,604`, `4 .184`); these are removed here and every figure is as printed. No source typos were corrected. The "$" that the PDF prints in its own column beside the first amount of a block is attached to the amount (`$12,450,431`). Indentation of sub-items on PDF p. 21 (rows 24-28) is not shown. Table column headings that the PDF prints over several rows or as spanning groups are written out as one heading per column in a single header row._

_Extractor note (not source text): Arithmetic cross-checks of the printed figures. The rows of PDF pp. 23-24 reproduce the printed relationships to rounding (column E = C / D in cents per kWh, GET revenue G = C * F / (1 - F), the percentage column B against column A). Printed totals differ slightly from sums of the printed rows, as the exhibit's rounding note anticipates: billed sales on PDF pp. 23 and 24 sum to 20,401,624 against a printed total of 20,401,626; the Rate 1 through Rate 119 revenue rows on PDF p. 23 sum to 810,979 against a printed 810,980; the (C) column on PDF p. 24 sums to 853,601 against a printed `$853,604`._
