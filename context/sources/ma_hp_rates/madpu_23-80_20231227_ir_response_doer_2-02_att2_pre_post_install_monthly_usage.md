# Docket 23-80: DOER 2-02 Attachment 2, Pre- and Post-Installation Monthly Usage

Attachment 2 to Unitil's Dec 27, 2023 response to DOER 2-02(b), which asked for pre- and post-installation monthly usage of customers with Mass Save heat pump rebates; DPU staff questioned Unitil's witness on it at the Feb 5, 2024 hearing. Two tabs list 264 electric and 51 gas customers with 12 months of kWh before and after install. Keep it as measured heat pump usage and rebate-tracked customer data.

## Sheets

- **Electric** (265 rows x 37 columns): Measure, Customer Name, Account Number, Service Address, Service City, Service State, Service Zip Code, Intall Date, Rate Class, PreInstallDate(-12), PreInstallDate(-11), PreInstallDate(-10), ...
- **Gas** (52 rows x 38 columns): Measure, Customer Name, Account Number, Service Address, Service City, Service State, Service Zip Code, Install Date, Rate Class, PreInstallDate(-12), PreInstallDate(-11), PreInstallDate(-10), ...
