# Instant Incentive Program Participation Dataset

The New England Heat Pump Accelerator publishes this workbook so partners can track its instant incentive program, open since February 2026. EXT Claims lists anonymous equipment claims by state, fuel, and install date, paid through September 30, 2026. Cited in CIEC-1: the 4,999 Connecticut claims overlap EnergizeCT and include other utilities, so the enrollment floor stays with the Company's count, and the monthly pace matches the ISO-NE ceiling.

## Sheets

- **EXT Claims** (25805 rows x 31 columns): Claim ID, Contractor Index, Manufacturer Index, Distributor Index, Customer State, Customer County, Building Type, Project Type, Previous Heating Fuel Type, Home Area Median Household Income ($K), Program Name, Sales Date, ...
