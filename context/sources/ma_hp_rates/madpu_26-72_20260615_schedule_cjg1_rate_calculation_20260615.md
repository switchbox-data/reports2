# Docket 26-72: Schedule CJG-1 (Rate Calculation)

Schedule CJG-1 of Unitil's June 15, 2026 compliance filing in D.P.U. 26-72, answering the DPU's June 1, 2026 order (25-08-A) to add winter transmission charges to its heat pump rates. The workbook spreads the residential transmission target over winter sales scaled by a 2.80 heat pump factor, giving summer and winter charges. Keep it for the mechanics of a seasonal transmission charge that assumes, not observes, heat pump load.
