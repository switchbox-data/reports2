# Docket 23-150: Attachment LI-NG-1-8-2, Average Monthly Usage by Heat Pump Program Enrollment

Attachment 2 to National Grid's response to information request LI-NG-1-8 in D.P.U. 23-150, which asked for usage of air source heat pump and electric resistance heat customers; the April 3, 2024 revised response still cites it. One values-only sheet gives 2021 to 2023 average monthly kWh for R-1 and R-2 customers in its 2019 to 2020 heat pump program versus others. The only measured heat pump usage National Grid has.

## Sheets

- **Sheet 1** (49 rows x 7 columns): Average Monthly Usage (kWh)
