# Docket 2021-00325: Attachment 2, Residential Rate Design Proposal and Illustrative Bill Impacts

CMP's Attachment 2 to its Apr. 21, 2022 response to Commission Staff's Apr. 7 Straw Proposal in the T&D rate design investigation (Docket 2021-00325). Three sheets: marginal-cost-based residential rates beside Rate A distribution rates, and illustrative monthly bills by usage level. We keep it as CMP's marginal-cost fixed-charge reasoning for supporting heat pump load; it is not heat-pump-specific and identifies no heat pump customers.

## Sheets

- **Rate A All Hours** (38 rows x 18 columns): Central Maine Power Company
- **Res All Hours** (38 rows x 18 columns): Central Maine Power Company
- **RES Bill Impacts T KWH** (63 rows x 51 columns): Central Maine Power Company
