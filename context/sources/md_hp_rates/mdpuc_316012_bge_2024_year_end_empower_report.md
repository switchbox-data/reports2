## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Program Participation and Impact Highlights

#### Program Impacts

BGE’s EmPOWER Maryland (“EmPOWER”) portfolio of programs continued to perform well throughout 2024. For 2024 overall, BGE’s EmPOWER program portfolio has exceeded its 2% goal legislatively established for the EmPOWER programs for 2024. In 2024, the EmPOWER program portfolio achieved 115% of the 2% annual MWh savings goal using 63% of the overall 2024 program budget. With regard to the MWh savings targets established in the Company’s 2024-2026 EmPOWER cycle filing, the portfolio achieved 114% of the 2024 filed target for the year. This places the portfolio in the “Exceeded Target” category for 2024. The entire portfolio achieved nearly 736,000 MWh in annualized electric energy savings. Natural gas savings in 2024 were over 2.7 million therms. In addition to the energy savings realized and associated environmental benefits customers generated by choosing more efficient alternatives, BGE customers received incentives and bill credits of approximately $152 million. Estimated savings on customer bills over the life of measures installed in 2024 are $503 million. Total economic benefits returned to BGE customers are summarized below.

| BGE EmPOWER Maryland Economic Customer Benefits – Year 2024 | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Direct rebates paid to customers for energy efficiency measures | $127,000,000 |
| Demand Response (excl. SER) customer credits and signing bonuses paid to customers | $22,000,000 |
| Smart Energy Rewards (SER) program credits paid to customers | $3,000,000 |
| Value of PeakRewards devices installed in customers' homes | $4,000,000 |
| Lifecycle savings to customers over the useful life of energy efficiency measures | $503,000,000 |
| Total value to BGE customers | $659,000,000 |

Avoided generation from the nearly 736,000 MWh 2024 portfolio savings over the estimated life of the energy efficiency measures and services installed in 2024 is nearly 4.4 million MWh.

### Program Highlights

While many programs exceeded their 2024 targets, there are four programs that have performed extremely well in 2024 that are worth noting. Outside of these four programs, for 2024, the Behavioral programs have also been very successful. The four programs mentioned below, and the behavioral programs, will be discussed in greater detail in the “Program Specifics” section of this report.

The following energy efficiency programs in the residential portfolio were very successful in 2024:

[PDF p. 6 | printed p. 2]

**HVAC:** The HVAC and HVAC Tune-up program performed well in 2024, achieving 139% of the 2024 MWh target. In 2024 the HVAC Heating and Cooling Program continued its focus on customer outreach, program awareness, and continuing to build relationships with distributors and HVAC contractors, which helped the program succeed.

**Residential New Construction (New Homes):** The Residential New Construction Program achieved strong results in 2024, with projects such as the first community of all-electric homes in Maryland constructed to EPA’s ENERGY STAR® NextGen Home certification requirements, which helped the program attain this goal. For 2024, the New Homes Program attained 130% of the 2024 goal.

The following commercial energy efficiency programs exceeded target expectations for 2024:

**Small Business:** In 2024, the Small Business Program has continued its success. The continued use of the BGE model of having a “closed” direct install network of six dedicated direct service providers continues to be a significant advantage for the program. For 2024, the program achieved 148% of its 2024 target.

**Combined Heat and Power:** For 2024, the Combined Heat and Power Program achieved 213% of its 2024 MWh savings target. This program recorded these higher savings as a result of efforts to close out various CHP projects in advance of the sunsetting of this program.

Effective January 1, 2024, the Smart Thermostat Rebate Program was incorporated into the Appliance Rebate Program to align with the delivery structure of the other Maryland Utilities. BGE offers a rebate of up to $100 to residential customers who purchase an ENERGY STAR® certified smart thermostat through one of several channels in the Appliance Rebate Program. Customers can receive an instant rebate on their purchase through BGE’s Online Marketplace or an instant coupon at select retailers. The program also offers a direct-to-consumer limited-time offer (LTO) to targeted customers through a simple-to-use online platform.

BGE also offers additional residential smart thermostat rebates through the HVAC Residential Midstream, New Homes, Quick Home Energy Check-up (“QHEC”), and Home Performance with ENERGY STAR® (“HPwES”) programs. Residential customers are eligible for up to three smart thermostat rebates (per program cycle) across all residential programs. BGE claims additional energy savings through cloud optimization services offered through eligible devices claimed through the programs mentioned above.

BGE’s Small Business Energy Solutions (“SBES”) Program offers smart thermostats to eligible commercial customers. Smart thermostat capabilities make the measure ideal for small businesses because there is an opportunity to significantly reduce load when the building is unoccupied.

##### Appliance Recycling

The Appliance Recycling Program provides residential customers the opportunity to recycle their
working inefficient appliances. During the three-year program cycle, customers can turn in
three large appliances (refrigerators or freezers) for a $50 rebate per unit and four small appliances
(room air conditioners or room dehumidifiers) for a $25 rebate per unit.

To turn in a small unit during a standard home pick-up appointment, customers must also recycle a
large unit. BGE offers customers two ways to participate if they only have small appliance(s).
Customers can either drop off their appliance(s) at various drop-off events held throughout the year
or BGE offers limited time offers (LTOs) for small unit only customers.

Below Target: BGE’s Appliance Recycling Program achieved 54% of the 2024 gross MWh
savings goal.

[PDF p. 28 | printed p. 24]

recycling vendor. The program had been paused since August of 2023 when the prior vendor ceased
business operations. Despite the delayed start, the program experienced a successful second half of
the year.

- Implemented two small unit focused LTOs in August and October. These LTOs allowed
  customers to recycle a dehumidifier independent of a large appliance. BGE customers were
  eligible to receive an additional $25 incentive (totaling $50) per dehumidifier recycled
  during the specified periods.
- Hosted two drop-off events in the BGE service territory in September and October to collect
  room air conditioners, dehumidifiers, and compact refrigerators.
- Conducted training with The Home Depot appliance sales associates on how to schedule

  newly purchased appliance.

##### Quick Home Energy Check-Up

Under the Quick Home Energy Check-Up (“QHEC”) Program, a Building Performance Institute
(“BPI”)-certified technician conducts a walk-through of a customer’s home and visually inspects

##### Home Performance with ENERGY STAR®

Home Performance with ENERGY STAR® (“HPwES”) is a national program overseen by the
Department of Energy and guided by the standards of the BPI. The program’s goal is to enhance
residential comfort and energy efficiency by addressing the house as a system, while improving the
health and safety of its occupants. Participating home performance contractors conduct
comprehensive audits and make customized recommendations designed to improve the comfort
and efficiency of the BGE customer’s home. These recommendations are provided in an audit
report that summarizes and prioritizes the opportunities for improvement. The customer decides
which measures they want installed and contracts with the participating contractor to complete the
job. The final project inspection and diagnostic testing ensure the contracted work was completed
properly. The program provides a subsidy for the cost of the home energy audit (customer pays
$100 for an audit valued at $400), and a rebate up to $10,000 for qualifying energy efficiency
improvements.

##### Energy Solutions for Business Program

The Energy Solutions for Business Program provides C&I customers with simple solutions to
improve the energy efficiency of their facilities with the goal of producing verifiable savings from
the adoption of more efficient technologies. This program consists of a dual-track approach. The
first is the Prescriptive Program, which offers a solution for C&I customers interested in purchasing
efficient electric technologies, such as lighting and controls, HVAC equipment, variable frequency
drives, and commercial kitchen & refrigeration equipment. The second track is the Custom Program
which, using site-specific custom measures, offers individually calculated incentives based on
specific project energy and demand savings. Since all customer sizes and sectors are eligible for
both of these programs, the program design reflects the flexibility necessary to serve the different
sectors and market players within the C&I market.

###### Commercial and Industrial Instant Discount (Midstream) Program

The C&I Instant Discount (Commercial Midstream) Program incentivizes C&I businesses to
choose efficient options when purchasing equipment through a distributor. This program seeks to
have distributors inform commercial businesses that there are energy-efficient options available for
lighting, HVAC, and other equipment and provide instant discounts “at the time of order” to
incentivize customers to purchase these efficient options. One of the most attractive features of this
program is that it provides discounts at the time of purchase without additional applications,
preauthorization requirements, or waiting periods.

Exceeded Target: For 2024 in its entirety the program realized 48,329 MWh representing 105%
of its annual goal. For the second half of 2024, the C&I Midstream program achieved 26,878 MWh
savings.

While operating smoothly, the HVAC portion of the Instant Discount Program continues to lag
behind the lighting component primarily due to continuing HVAC industry supply issues,
increasing efficiency standards, and incentives that do not entirely make up the price differential.
HVAC results are reported as a part of the overall program results.

PeakRewards Energy Savings (MWh)

The as filed Annualized Energy Savings for PeakRewards was made up of two components:

- Annualized Energy Savings (MWh) from installed smart thermostats. These energy savings
  are not associated with PeakRewards events; and,
- Energy savings coincident with the hours during PeakRewards calls.
  Prior to 2018, Commission Staff required a mini-table, the DRDP Call Log, which included energy
  savings associated with the PeakRewards events. The current cycle DRDP Call Log eliminated
  MWh savings but instead includes MW reductions.

Annualized Energy Savings from smart thermostats.

not report energy savings coincident with event days.

Residential Lighting Program
Participant: A household that purchases discounted screw-in light-emitting diode (“LED”) bulbs

Residential Appliance Rebate Program
Participant: Downstream Retail Sales – A residential customer or a landlord of a residential
customer who purchases a qualified appliance and submits a rebate application form.
Note: If a customer purchases an appliance and submits a rebate application, that customer
is counted as one participant. If that rebate application is for multiple appliances,
that customer is still counted as a single participant. However, if later that same
customer purchases another appliance and submits a new rebate application, they
will be counted as another participant.

Midstream Retailer Incentives – Retailer sales through the ENERGY STAR® Retail
Products Program (“ESRPP”) are incentivized with a payment to the retailer for
stocking the qualifying ENERGY STAR® measure. Every qualifying measure sold
that is incentivized by BGE counts as a single participant.

Any qualifying instant or distributor rebate given at the register and funded by BGE
also qualifies as a single participant.

Measures: Qualified appliances under the program (clothes washers, electric clothes dryers,
hybrid heat pump water heaters, pool pumps, refrigerators, and dehumidifiers)

Residential Appliance Recycling Program
Participant: A residential customer who requests removal of a qualified refrigerator, freezer, or
room air conditioner (“A/C”) from home (the latter if already removing a refrigerator
or freezer).

Note: If a customer recycles an appliance and receives an incentive, that customer is
counted as one participant. If that incentive is for multiple appliances, that customer
is still counted as a single participant. However, if later that same customer recycles
another appliance and receives an additional rebate, they will be counted as another
participant.

Measure: Qualified appliances in working condition - refrigerator and/or freezer,
dehumidifier, and room air conditioner. Limit of three large units (refrigerator
and/or freezer) and four small units (room air conditioners and/or dehumidifiers) per
BGE residential household.
