# Electric vehicle rates and programs: catalog of what we know (Northeast and Mid-Atlantic)

**Last updated:** 2026-10-27. **Scope:** residential EV time-of-use rates, separately metered EV rates, off-peak charging rebates, managed-charging programs, and the commercial EV demand-charge relief rates that sit alongside them, at utilities from Maine to West Virginia. Commercial and public-charging rates are included when a utility's EV offering is mostly commercial, so a reader knows it is not a residential option.

This file answers three questions for each offering: what is it, **which docket defined it**, and **how do we know**. It starts from the NEEP survey ([`../../sources/neep_modern_rate_design.md`](../../sources/neep_modern_rate_design.md), Appendix A and E) and goes to primary documents where it could. See also [`time_varying_rates.md`](time_varying_rates.md) (whole-home TOU rates that EV owners can also use) and [`electric_heating_and_heat_pump_rates.md`](electric_heating_and_heat_pump_rates.md).

## How to read this catalog

**Category codes:** EV-TOU (rate or supply credit that varies by time for EV charging); EV-other (rebates, managed charging, demand-charge relief, separate-meter provisions); other. Where a utility has no EV-specific rate, the entry says so.

- **Defining docket:** the proceeding that created or approved the offering. "Not found" means we have not located it, not that it does not exist.
- **Current governing docket:** the proceeding named on today's tariff footer or behind the current rate order, or where the offering is being changed. Tariff footers usually cite this one, which is why the defining docket is often hard to find.
- **Docket status** in the index table: _found_ = a defining docket is named; _partial_ = a revising or continuing docket is confirmed but the original creator is not; _unverified_ = named from a secondary source or inferred; _not found_.
- **Evidence level:** _Primary_ = the order, testimony, tariff text, or statute was read. _Official page_ = a utility or agency web page. _Search summary_ = only a search snippet or synthesis was seen; spot-check before relying on it.
- Research was done on 2026-10-27 by web search and page fetch. Where an order was cited but not opened, the entry says so. Open items for every region are in [`README.md`](README.md).
