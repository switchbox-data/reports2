## Document overview

This filing is National Grid's compliance proposal in response to the DPU's D.P.U. 25-08-A order directing National Grid and Unitil to extend seasonal heat pump rate discounts to transmission charges, following the methodology approved for Eversource/NSTAR Electric in D.P.U. 25-55.

The document contains:

- **Exhibit 1** (pp. 1–2): The seasonal transmission rate design worksheet, showing how the winter HP transmission rate of $0.01998/kWh is derived from the flat rate of $0.05464/kWh
- **Exhibit 2** (pp. 3–6): Bill impact analysis for transmission-only HP rate (9% bill reduction)
- **Exhibit 3** (pp. 7–10): Bill impact analysis for combined distribution + transmission HP rate (20% bill reduction)
- **Tariff sheets** (pp. 11–35): Clean and redlined R-1 and R-2 tariff pages for Massachusetts Electric and Nantucket Electric
- **Transmission Service Cost Adjustment Provision** (pp. 36–39): Clean and redlined provision adding HP-specific transmission language

## Tariff Sheets: R-1 Residential Regular (Pages 11–17)

### Clean version (M.D.P.U. No. 1641, canceling 1634)

**Issued**: June 15, 2026 | **Effective**: November 1, 2026

**AVAILABILITY**: Electric delivery service for domestic purposes in individual private dwellings/apartments and for church and farm purposes.

**HEAT PUMP RATE**: All R-1 customers who have a device used for space heating or supplementing another space heating source during winter months by transferring thermal energy from the outside with the use of the refrigeration cycle are eligible to opt into the R-1 Heat Pump Rate. Under the R-1 Heat Pump Rate, customers will be charged the **R-1 Heat Pump Base Distribution Charge** and **R-1 Heat Pump Transmission Service Charge** based on their kWh usage during the **winter heating season, defined as November 1 through April 30**. For new customers, the R-1 Heat Pump Rate will be applied to the first bill following their enrollment.

Customers may return to the default rate after 12 billing cycles and must remain on the default rate for 12 billing cycles before re-enrolling.

**TRANSMISSION SERVICE COST ADJUSTMENT**: Customers eligible for the Heat Pump Rate shall be placed on a reduced R-1 Heat Pump Transmission Service Charge during the winter heating season. The transmission charge shall be calculated in accordance with the Company's Transmission Service Cost Adjustment Provision.

### Redlined version (M.D.P.U. No. 1634→1641, canceling 1609→1634)

Key changes from previous tariff:

- Added "R-1 Heat Pump **Transmission Service Charge**" alongside the existing Heat Pump Base Distribution Charge
- Eligibility language refined: "used ~~to heat~~ **for space heating** or ~~supplement~~ **supplementing** another ~~heat~~ **space heating** source"
- Added auto-enrollment: "Customers who previously applied for a heat pump rebate through one of the Company's or Boston Gas Company d/b/a National Grid's (together, 'National Grid') energy efficiency programs … will be **automatically enrolled** in the R-1 Heat Pump Rate." New rebate applicants will also be auto-enrolled on a roll-forward basis, with an opt-out option.
- Transmission section: Added sentence "Customers eligible for the Heat Pump Rate shall be placed on a reduced R-1 Heat Pump Transmission Service Charge during the winter heating season, as described above." Previous version only referenced distribution.

## Transmission Service Cost Adjustment Provision (Pages 36–39)

### Clean version (M.D.P.U. No. 1640, canceling 1592)

This provision governs how transmission costs are allocated and recovered from customers. Key language:

> "The Transmission Service Cost Adjustment shall collect from customers taking transmission service under the Company's rates the transmission costs billed to the Company by New England Power Company ('NEP'), any other transmission provider, the New England Power Pool, a regional transmission group, an independent system operator or any other entity that is authorized to bill the Company directly for transmission services."

The adjustment is calculated **separately for each rate class** as a **cents per kilowatt-hour factor** applicable to all kWh delivered.

**Heat Pump specific language**:

> "Rate R-1 and Rate R-2 residential customers who were placed on the Heat Pump Rate pursuant to the Rate R-1 and Rate R-2 retail delivery service provisions, subject to change from time to time, shall be placed on a **Heat Pump Transmission Service Charge**, which will be calculated using the methodology described below and **divided by the forecasted heat pump customers kilowatt hours for the winter season**, which is defined as November 1 through April 30."

The factor is established annually based on a forecast of transmission costs and includes a full reconciliation and adjustment for any over/under recovery.

### Redlined version (M.D.P.U. No. 1592→1640, canceling 1225→1592)

The redline adds the entire Heat Pump Transmission Service Charge paragraph (quoted above) to the existing provision. The previous version had no heat-pump-specific transmission language.
