# Low-income and affordability programs: catalog of what we know (Northeast and Mid-Atlantic)

**Last updated:** 2026-10-27. **Scope:** residential electric bill discounts, percentage-of-income payment plans, arrearage management, budget billing, customer-relief (hardship) funds, and state energy assistance (including LIHEAP) at utilities from Maine to West Virginia. It is the equivalent of NEEP's Appendix C, with dockets and primary sources added.

This file answers three questions for each program: what is it, **which docket (or statute) defined it**, and **how do we know**. It starts from the NEEP survey ([`../../sources/neep_modern_rate_design.md`](../../sources/neep_modern_rate_design.md), Appendix C and E). Many of these programs are statutory or run by a state agency outside the utility commission, so "docket" can mean a rulemaking, a statute, or a legislative appropriation. For how LMI discounts work in general, see the other files in [`../`](../) (for example the LMI and discount-rate documents in `context/domain/charges/`).

## How to read this catalog

**Category codes:** LI-discount (fixed or tiered bill discount); LI-PIPP (percentage-of-income payment plan, Pennsylvania-style CAP); LI-arrearage (arrearage management or forgiveness); LI-budget-billing (levelized payments); LI-customer-relief (hardship or charity funds); LI-state-assistance (LIHEAP and state-run programs, one-time bill credits and refunds); other.

- **Defining docket:** the proceeding that created or approved the offering. "Not found" means we have not located it, not that it does not exist.
- **Current governing docket:** the proceeding named on today's tariff footer or behind the current rate order, or where the offering is being changed. Tariff footers usually cite this one, which is why the defining docket is often hard to find.
- **Docket status** in the index table: _found_ = a defining docket is named; _partial_ = a revising or continuing docket is confirmed but the original creator is not; _unverified_ = named from a secondary source or inferred; _not found_.
- **Evidence level:** _Primary_ = the order, testimony, tariff text, or statute was read. _Official page_ = a utility or agency web page. _Search summary_ = only a search snippet or synthesis was seen; spot-check before relying on it.
- Research was done on 2026-10-27 by web search and page fetch. Where an order was cited but not opened, the entry says so. Open items for every region are in [`README.md`](README.md).

### Maine — Arrearage Management Program (AMP), CMP and Versant

- **Category:** LI-arrearage
- **Status (Oct 2026):** Active; statutory authority repealed 9/30/2028.
- **Design:** Forgiveness up to $500/month, $6,000/year for LIAP-eligible customers; 7-year re-entry. Versant: forgiveness up to 100% of the past-due amount, capped at $6,000.
- **Defining docket:** Statute 35-A §3214(2-A) (origin PL 2013 c.556; amended PL 2017 c.414, PL 2021 c.101, PL 2023 c.534). The original Chapter 317 rulemaking docket was NOT FOUND. _[partial]_
- **Current governing docket:** **2023-00134** (Chapter 317 amendment; opened 6/9/2023 on OPA petition; order 12/5/2023). Inquiry 2023-00239 (opened 9/13/2023); PUC AMP report to the Legislature 1/28/2024.
- **Where we learned it:** Primary
- **In NEEP:** yes (App C, CMP and Versant "AMP").
- **Notes:** —

## New Hampshire

### New Hampshire — statewide Electric Assistance Program (EAP; Eversource Rate EAP; Liberty, Unitil, NHEC)

- **Category:** LI-discount
- **Status (Oct 2026):** Active; 2026-27 program year administrative budgets are in EAP 2026-001 / DE 26-043.
- **Design:** Five discount tiers applied to the first 750 kWh per month, funded by the system benefits charge. From 10/1/2023 the tiers are 5%, 19%, 36%, 54%, 86% (previously 8%, 22%, 36%, 52%, 76%). The goal is average bills between 4% and 5% of income for each tier.
- **Defining docket:** NHPUC **Order No. 23,980 (May 30, 2002)**, which approved the statewide tiered discount program; rehearing denied in Order 24,013. The docket number was not confirmed (a search synthesis suggested DR 01-001; unverified). _[partial]_

- **In NEEP:** yes (App C, Eversource "Rate EAP"; NH DOE).
- **Notes:** In DE 22-043 the Advisory Board recommended a generic investigation of an AMP in EAP (Recommendation 5); I found no outcome.

### Unitil — Arrearage Management Program

- **Category:** LI-arrearage
