# Docket 23-80: DOER 2-02 Attachment 1, Heat Pump Measure IDs

Attachment 1 to Unitil's Dec 27, 2023 response to DOER 2-02(a), which asked how Unitil tracks customers who received Mass Save heat pump incentives. It lists the electric- and gas-division heat pump measures with their BCR Measure IDs, by fuel displaced and full or partial displacement. Keep it as the code list behind Unitil's statement that rebate Measure IDs let it find heat pump customers.

## Sheets

- **Electric Heat Pump measures** (79 rows x 5 columns): DPU 23-80 / DPU 23-81

- **Gas Heat Pump Measures** (48 rows x 5 columns): DPU 23-80 / DPU 23-81
