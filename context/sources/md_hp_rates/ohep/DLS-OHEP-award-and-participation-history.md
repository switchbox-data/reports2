Metrics (as labeled in the exhibits):

- **Applications** — total applied
- **Receiving Benefits** — households receiving benefits
- **Average Benefit** — average award `$`
- **Benefits Paid ($ in Millions)** — total award amount paid in the window

These are **not** full fiscal-year totals (except as otherwise noted elsewhere). FY 2021 is a shorter window (through November).

## Notes from the source exhibits

- **FY 2025 Jul–Dec averages omit year-end supplemental benefits.** 2027FY Exhibit 14 note: monthly FY 2025 data through December did not include the end-of-year supplemental; final FY 2025 averages after supplemental were higher (see 2027FY Exhibit 12: MEAP `$465`, EUSP `$549` including supplemental).
- **FY 2021 window** in 2023FY Exhibit 2 is July–November, not July–December.
