# Docket 26-154: Schedule SED-3, External Transmission Charge Reconciliation

Schedule SED-3 of Unitil's Nov. 2, 2026 annual reconciliation filing in D.P.U. 26-154: the monthly External Transmission Charge reconciliation for 2025 to 2027. The company-wide over- or under-recovery goes into 2027 costs, which are split by fixed 12-CP shares (RD-1/RD-2 45.15%). Read for who absorbs a Unitil transmission shortfall.
