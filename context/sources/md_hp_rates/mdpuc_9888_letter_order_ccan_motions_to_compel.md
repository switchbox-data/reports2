_**Re: Chesapeake Climate Action Network's Motions to Compel Responses to Certain Discovery Requests—Case No. 9888**_

On August 31, 2026 and September 2, 2026, the Chesapeake Climate Action Network ("CCAN") filed Motions to Compel ("Motions") requesting that the Commission issue an order directing Baltimore Gas and Electric Company ("BGE" or the "Company") to respond to Data Request Nos. ("DRs") 1-12 through 1-21, 2-1, and 2-2 in greater detail.[^1] On September 15, 2026, BGE filed a response objecting to CCAN's Motion on substantive and procedural grounds.[^2] For the reasons explained below, CCAN's Motions to Compel are granted in part and denied in part.

In its Motions, CCAN asserts that the information it requests is relevant to BGE's rate case and that the Company's objection to CCAN's request for information is impermissibly vague.[^3] CCAN argues that BGE utilized blanket objections to the DRs and failed to specify which aspects were invalid.[^4] CCAN states the DRs seek information that is directly relevant to the proper allocation of costs to different categories of customers, an issue of central importance in every rate case.[^5] Finally, CCAN proposes that the request will not pose undue burden or hardship and will provide relevant information to all parties.[^6]

[PDF p. 2]

In response to CCAN's Motions, BGE argues that they are unduly burdensome and beyond the scope of the proceeding.[^7] BGE estimates that it would take at least two months to produce the data CCAN is requesting, and would require the exclusive assignment of at least four distribution system planning engineers to this task.[^8] BGE notes that the Company does not evaluate cost allocation on the feeder or transformer level, as requested in CCAN's DRs.[^9] As such, the requested data on BGE's 1,549 feeders and 420 distribution substation transformers would produce irrelevant information to the immediate preceding [sic].[^10] Finally, BGE asserts that CCAN failed to "make every reasonable effort to resolve discovery disputes," as the Company first became aware of the dispute upon receiving the Motions.[^11]

After a careful review of CCAN's Motions and BGE's response, the Commission finds that the Motions should be granted in part and denied in part. The requested information must be sufficiently related to the issues presented in this proceeding, and the scope of discovery should be proportionate to the needs of the case. Although information concerning BGE's electric distribution system may generally be relevant to understanding cost allocation, system planning, and distribution investments, CCAN has not established that all of the system-wide information requested is necessary to evaluate the investments or other issues presented in this proceeding.

The Commission will therefore require BGE to provide certain feeder- and substation-transformer-level information to the extent that the information relates to investments currently under review in this proceeding. Limiting production to feeders and transformers associated with investments under review also addresses BGE's concerns regarding the substantial burden associated with producing system-wide information.

Specifically, the Commission directs BGE to respond to CCAN's DRs 1-13, 1-14, 1-16, 1-17, 2-1, and 2-2 only for investments currently under review during this rate case. With respect to CCAN's DRs 1-12 and 1-15, the Commission directs BGE to respond only regarding investments currently under review during this rate case for subparts a-e, but not for subparts f-h, which goes beyond the scope of this case. In regard to CCAN's DRs 1-18 and 1-19, the Commission denies CCAN's request as CCAN fails to articulate how this request is related to evaluating investments for which BGE is seeking recovery in this rate case. Concerning CCAN's DRs 1-20 and 1-21, the Commission directs BGE to respond to those DRs because they relate to relevant utility planning criteria necessary for evaluating investments. See Attachment A for the Commission's decisions

[PDF p. 3]

on CCAN's DRs for additional information. The substance of CCAN's DRs and BGE's responses can be discussed in rebuttal or surrebuttal testimony.
