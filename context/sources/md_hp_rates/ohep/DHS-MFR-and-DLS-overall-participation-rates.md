## A. By-program overall take-up (recipients ÷ income-eligible)

These are the clearest “share of eligible households receiving MEAP / EUSP” figures in the older DLS books. They are **not** published the same way for FY2021–FY2025 in the packets above.

### Fiscal 2017 (APPRISE / OPC market characterization, cited in DLS FY2020 Exhibit 3)

APPRISE estimated **>380,000** income-eligible OHEP households; about **one-quarter** received assistance.

| Income band | EUSP | MEAP |
| ----------- | ---: | ---: |
| 0–75% FPL | 29% | 29% |
| 76–110% FPL | 31% | 31% |
| 111–150% FPL | 24% | 24% |
| 151–175% FPL | 14% | 15% |
| **All income-eligible** | **25%** | **26%** |

Jurisdiction extremes (same report / DLS FY2020): Allegany & Garrett **46%** (both programs); Montgomery **15% MEAP / 14% EUSP**.

### Federal fiscal 2016–2018 (LIHEAP heating assistance — DLS FY2021)

Maryland LIHEAP heating participation (state eligibility criteria): **25%–27%** each year; 10th-highest among states in FFY2018 (national ~17%).

### Fiscal 2017–2019 (DLS FY2021)

DLS notes that in DHS’s annual MFR submission (State fiscal year), **participation rates remained at essentially the same levels through fiscal 2019** as the APPRISE ~25–26% figures.

### Fiscal 2020 (DLS FY2022)

> “the share of the eligible population receiving each benefit type declined by 2 percentage points (**25% for MEAP**, **25% for EUSP bill payment assistance**, and **3% for EUSP arrearage assistance**). Participation rates had held relatively level in the preceding three years.”

So: FY2020 ≈ **25% / 25%**; prior three years ≈ **~27%** for MEAP and EUSP bill payment.

### FY2021–FY2025

**No matching by-program overall %** (MEAP / EUSP bill recipients ÷ estimated eligible) appears in DLS FY2023–FY2027 Performance Analysis or in DHS MFR FY2023–FY2026 Obj. 2.5 tables. DLS FY2027 explicitly says the FY2027 MFR submission **omitted** general participation-rate measures; DHS agreed to restore them (by program) with the FY2028 MFR for FY2026.

## B. Related MFR metric — unified applications from eligible households

DHS MFR Obj. 2.5 reports **“Percent of OHEP unified applications received and processed from eligible households.”** This is **not** the same as recipients ÷ eligible (it is application-processing reach). Compiled from MFR FY2023–FY2026 packets (actuals only):

| Year | Unified apps processed from eligible households |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------: |
| 2017 | 40.0% |
| 2018 | 40.0% |
| 2019 | 39.9% |
| 2020 | 38.0% |
| 2021 | 40.7% |
| 2022 | **65.0%** |
| 2023 | 41.0% |
| 2024 | 39.0% |

(MFR FY2026 also lists FY2024 MEAP enrollments **94,195** and EUSP **110,146**, with FY2025/FY2026 *estimates* of 250k–270k — planning figures, not realized.)

## C. Vulnerable-population MFR rates (subset take-up)

Still published: % of **Census-estimated eligible households in each subgroup** that receive energy assistance. Prefer the **DLS narrative year labels** when packets disagree after methodology updates (DHS FY2027 testimony notes a 2025 methodology change and limited back-revision).

**FY2023** (DLS FY2025 Exhibit 1 narrative): age 60+ **24.7%**; disabled **21.6%**; child under 6 **36.6%**.

**FY2025** (DLS FY2027 Exhibit 1 narrative): age 60+ **39.1%**; disabled **30.9%**; child under 6 **18.0%**.

Earlier MFR actuals (from DHS MFR tables; column years as labeled in each packet) are useful for trend context but treat FY2023↔FY2025 comparisons cautiously after DHS’s methodology note.

## Notes for modeling

- Best historical **overall** MEAP/EUSP take-up anchors in these pulls: **~25–27%** (FY2017 APPRISE; ~FY2017–2019 stable; FY2020 25%/25%).
- Switchbox’s **~48%** figure for FY2025 uses DHS’s **270,000** planning eligible count ÷ ~130k unique recipients — a different denominator construction than the APPRISE ACS-based ~380k income-eligible pool.
- Expect restored by-program overall rates in **FY2028 MFR** (per DHS concurrence on DLS committee narrative).
