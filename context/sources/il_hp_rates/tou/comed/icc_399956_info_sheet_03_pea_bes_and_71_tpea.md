**Reading guide (added by extractor, not source text)**: The PEA (Rate BES) and TPEA (Rate BEST) are the monthly true-ups that reconcile ComEd's procurement expense to revenue for each bundled supply rate. Both appear on the bill as "Purchased Electricity Adjustment." For June–July 2026 the flat-rate PEA is 0.230 then 0.247 ¢/kWh, while the Rate BEST TPEA is 0.000 in both months.
