# Heat Pump Installations, Excluding Water Heaters

Eversource Connecticut publishes this table from the Connecticut Energy Efficiency Dashboard for its Conservation and Load Management programs. It counts residential heat pump installations for Eversource Electric by year and equipment type from 2017 through 2026, leaving out heat pump water heaters. CIEC-1 uses it in place of the November 5 export, which included water heaters.

## Sheets

- **Export** (14 rows x 14 columns): Year, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026, Total
- **Detail** (74 rows x 6 columns): Company, Sector, Heat Pump Type, Sub Program, Year, Installations
