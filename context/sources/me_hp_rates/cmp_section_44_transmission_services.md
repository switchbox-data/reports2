## Key Insight for SHP Rate

The A-SEASONAL transmission rate of $0.110929/kWh was applied **only during May through October**. This means:

- **Winter (Nov–Apr):** $0.00/kWh transmission — heat pump customers paid zero transmission charges during heating months
- **Summer (May–Oct):** $0.110929/kWh transmission — all annual transmission costs were concentrated into 6 summer months

For comparison, Rate A charges $0.047973/kWh transmission year-round. The SHP rate's summer-only transmission at $0.110929 is approximately 2.31× the Rate A annual transmission rate, designed to collect roughly equivalent annual transmission revenue from a customer with flat usage across seasons while removing all transmission charges during the heating season.
