## C. Program Descriptions

### ChargeSmart (Electric Vehicle Charging Program, Rider EVCP)

| Field                   | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Objective               | The provisions within the ChargeSmart Program are intended to encourage efficient use of the distribution system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Target Market           | The ChargeSmart Program includes offerings for both residential and non-residential customers. For residential offerings, the program targets all residential customers who own an EV and have the ability to charge at home. For non-residential offerings, the program targets: multifamily facilities, education facilities, transit facilities, public charging facilities, and fleet facilities that are classified as DS-2, DS-3, or DS-4 customers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Description             | For residential customers, the ChargeSmart Program includes an opt-in EV rate that encourages charging at times better for the grid with bill credits and Preferred Charging Period Delivery Credit. This is offered to all DS1 customers. For non-residential customers, the opt-in ChargeSmart Program encourages charging at times beneficial to the grid with a Preferred Charging Period Delivery Credit and a monthly EV Bill Credit. For non-residential all DS-3/DS-4 facilities, demand charges will not be billed during off-peak periods. The rate limiter is applicable to Public Charging Facility customers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Implementation Strategy | Implementation activities will focus on enhancing ChargeSmart program operations and marketing to increase awareness and further support customers. Ameren Illinois will monitor customer awareness, understanding, and satisfaction with participation in ChargeSmart through periodic customer surveys. The program implementation strategy will be geared towards engaging customers and driving towards participation goals.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Offerings & Incentives  | For details on the rate, refer to the latest Rider EVCP – Optional Electric Vehicle Charging Program Tariff (Please see the AIC Rider EVCP Qualifying Electric Vehicle Facility Specifications). Below is a summary of the rate provisions. For residential customers and DS-2 non-residential, there are two main incentives in the ChargeSmart Program: (1) **Delivery Credit or Charge** – encourages charging at times beneficial to the grid with Preferred Charging Period Delivery Credit for every kWh used. **Preferred Charging Period (PCP): 11:00 p.m. – 7:00 a.m.** A credit for your electric delivery service is given for every kWh of usage during this period. **Non-Preferred Charging Period (NPCP): 11:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.** During the highest one hour of household usage from the billing period that occurs within this timeframe, there will be an additional charge for each kWh used. **Morning and Evening: 7:00 a.m. – 11:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. – 11:00 p.m.** There are no additional credits or charges for electric usage during this time period. (2) **Bill Credit** – includes a monthly EV Bill Credit for the first 12 consecutive months. First-Year Bill Credit: to offset potentially higher charges for usage during the NPCP, Ameren Illinois offers a monthly bill credit for your first 12 months enrolled on the EV Rate Program. The program also offers the following components for non-residential customers: Off-Peak Demand Charges Eliminated (DS-3/DS-4 facility types: Fleet, Transit, Education, Public Charging); Rate Limiter Credits (all public charging facilities). |

### Residential FlexCharge

| Field                   | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Objective               | The FlexCharge Program aims to enhance grid reliability and optimize energy resources through effective management of EV charging activities within the Ameren Illinois service territory.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Target Market           | Residential customers with Level 1 & Level 2 Chargers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Description             | The FlexCharge Program will allow Ameren Illinois to actively control EV loads on the electric system, manage peak conditions, and minimize grid impacts. Customers will receive an incentive for participating while also gaining knowledge about when are the best times to charge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Implementation Strategy | The managed charging program will be continuing from BE Plan 2. Ameren Illinois will work with third party vendor(s) to conduct the following activities: customer recruitment and enrollment; provide a software/hardware solution and ensure the solution operates appropriately; establish and maintain connectivity with chargers/EVs; collect and provide data, as prescribed by Ameren Illinois; provide comprehensive performance analysis, lessons learned, and recommendations. Ameren Illinois will implement an optimized charging program that prioritizes charging when it's cost effective and minimally impactful to the grid. Customers will be able to set a target charge level and ready-by time, and a vendor implemented program will control when to charge. The FlexCharge program may shift control strategies to ensure optimizations are met based on customer feedback, vendor capabilities, and future integrations with other programs. |
| Offerings & Incentives  | Customers will receive a one-time incentive of $50 upon enrollment and a monthly incentive of $10 for on-going participation in the programs. Customers will be able to opt out of or unenroll from the program at any time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
