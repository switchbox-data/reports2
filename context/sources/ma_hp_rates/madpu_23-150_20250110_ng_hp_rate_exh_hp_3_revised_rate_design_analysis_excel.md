# Docket 23-150: Exhibit HP-3-Revised, Heat Pump Rate Calculations and Bill Impacts

Exhibit HP-3-Revised, enclosed with National Grid's January 10, 2025 answers to DPU questions on its December 30, 2024 heat pump rate compliance filing in D.P.U. 23-150; it replaces the original HP-3. Five sheets of live formulas derive the R-1/R-2 winter kWh charge from a class revenue target and a 2.8 usage multiplier, with bill impacts. Shows how a heat pump rate is priced without a list of heat pump customers.

## Sheets

- **R-1_R-2_Heat-Pump Rate** (90 rows x 18 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **R-1 Factor & Bill Impacts** (42 rows x 7 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **R-2 Factor & Bill Impacts** (43 rows x 7 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Resi Typical Bill (3 Ton HP)** (67 rows x 9 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Resi Typical Bill (5 Ton HP)** (67 rows x 9 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
