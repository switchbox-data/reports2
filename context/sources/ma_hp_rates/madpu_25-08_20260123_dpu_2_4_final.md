**Information Request:**

Refer to Petition, Exhibit D. Please explain whether the Company collects the data needed to report on the percentage of customers eligible for the seasonal heat pump rate who are enrolled in it. As part of this response, describe any costs, operational challenges, or other barriers associated with tracking and reporting this data.

**Response:**

The Company is enrolling or has enrolled all customers that it knows are eligible for the heat pump rate. The Company has no means of collecting data on how many additional customers in the Commonwealth have an eligible heat pump but have not yet enrolled, and therefore the Company cannot report such a metric.

Before the launch of the rate on November 1, 2025, the Company automatically enrolled all customers who had received a heat pump from a Mass Save Sponsor after January 1, 2019. Subsequent to the launch of the rate, the Company has been automatically enrolling all new customers receiving Mass Save rebates for heat pumps on a weekly basis.

Additionally, nearly 12,000 customers who were not captured in those processes (because they did not receive a rebate from a Mass Save Sponsor, or installed prior to 2019) have submitted the necessary documentation and been manually enrolled on the rate.
