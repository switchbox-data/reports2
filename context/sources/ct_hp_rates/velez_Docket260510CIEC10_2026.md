# Docket 26-05-10: CIEC-10(a) Workpaper, Hourly Electric Resistance Backup Load

Workpaper supporting CLF-SC's response to CIEC-10(a) in the Eversource Connecticut rate case. Gives the average fossil fuel-heated home's modeled hourly electric resistance backup, heat pump, and whole-home load after the retrofit for all 8,760 hours, with annual totals and peak hours as formulas. Read for when and how much backup heat the testimony's modeled heat pumps use.

## Sheets

- **README** (12 rows x 1 columns): CIEC-10(a) workpaper
- **hourly_load** (8761 rows x 9 columns): timestamp, weather_hour_2018, backup_kwh, heat_pump_kwh, whole_home_kwh, heat_pump_plus_backup_kwh
