# Docket 23-80: Exhibit Unitil-JDT, Rate Design Model Electric (Public Version)

Unitil's public rate design model, filed with its responses to DPU information requests (sets 12 and 13) in the 23-80 rate case; the latest of three public versions. It holds sheets behind Exhibits JDT-4 to JDT-9, including a 'Heat Pump Rate Design' sheet with live formulas converting gas-heating therms to heat pump kWh. Use it as a template for a revenue-neutral heat pump rate designed without identifying heat pump customers.
