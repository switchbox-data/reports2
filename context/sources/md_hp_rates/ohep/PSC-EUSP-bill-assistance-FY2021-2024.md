**Programs**: Electric Universal Service Program (EUSP) — bill payment assistance only (not arrearage)
**Publisher**: Maryland Public Service Commission (statutory EUSP annual reports under PUA §7-512.1)
**Compiled from**:

Figures below are **full program-year** EUSP **bill assistance** (not arrearage): households served, total benefit expense, and average grant per household.

---

## EUSP bill assistance — full-year series

| Fiscal year | Households served | Total benefit expense | Average grant |
| ----------- | ----------------: | --------------------: | ------------: |
| FY 2021     |            83,702 |         $41.8 million |          $504 |
| FY 2022     |            79,951 |   $60.8–$61.0 million |          $763 |
| FY 2023     |            96,367 |         $90.4 million |          $939 |
| FY 2024     |           114,230 |         $71.6 million |          $628 |

Sources: Table 1 in each PSC report listed above (FY 2022 expense appears as $60.8M or $60.964M depending on the report).

---

## Notes

- Average grant ≈ total benefit expense ÷ households served.
- These are **realized** average awards, not the published poverty-level × usage **benefit matrix**.
- MEAP is mentioned in the 2024 report (e.g. MEAP benefit expenditures ~$68.0M in FY 2024 plus smaller SNAP-related grants) but the PSC Table 1 series above is EUSP-only.
