## Plain Language Summary

On October 30, 2025 [sic; the filing is dated October 30, 2026] NSTAR Electric Company ("Company") filed with the Department of Public Utilities its annual electric Revenue Decoupling Adjustment Factor ("RDAF"), which will go into effect beginning January 1, 2027. The RDAF is a mechanism by which the Company balances the difference between the amount Eversource is allowed to collect for energy delivery during a specific period for its operating costs, and how much was actually collected at the end of that period.

If approved by the Department of Public Utilities, Company's RDAF will save residential customers approximately $3.36 per month on their bill as compared to the current rate in effect.

The above plain language summary is provided for informational purposes only and is not intended to replace any official notice issued by the Department in this matter. Consistent with the Tier 3 Outreach Plan, a copy of the official Notice of Proceeding will be posted to the Company's website.

## Tier 3 Outreach Plan

The Department has classified Revenue Decoupling Adjustment Factor filings as a Tier 3 Proceeding. Consistent with that designation, the Company will plan to conduct the following outreach:

- To publish the Notice of Filing issued in this proceeding prominently on the Company's website;[^1]
- To serve a copy of the Notice of Filing on any required service lists as dictated by the Department of Public Utilities; and
- Any other publication or outreach that the Department of Public Utilities may require.

Consistent with outreach previously conducted, the Company will provide a copy of the Notice of Proceeding to any other individuals who have requested to receive information on the Company's filings. The Company will list this proceeding on its website as directed by the Department.

## Introduction

NSTAR Electric Company d/b/a Eversource Energy (the "Company") presents for filing its annual electric Revenue Decoupling Adjustment Factor ("RDAF"), applicable to all of the Company's delivery service rate schedules. The Company is filing its RDAF, proposed for effect January 1, 2027, pursuant to the Massachusetts Department of Public Utilities' ("Department") Order in D.P.U. 22-22, and in compliance with the Company's Revenue Decoupling Adjustment Mechanism ("RDAM") tariff, M.D.P.U. No. 60N.

The Department established standard uniform revenue decoupling filing requirements for natural gas and electric distribution companies in its Order in D.P.U. 14-RDAF-01 dated December 9, 2014 and in D.P.U.21-RDAF-01 dated January 27, 2021. The Company has listed these filing requirements as Schedules A through H in the Index to this filing. Additionally, the Company has included other information as required in Section 1.08 of the RDAM tariff, M.D.P.U. No. 60N as Schedule J. The Company is also including Schedule L and Schedule M for heat pump reporting, in compliance with the Orders issued in D.P.U. 25-08 and D.P.U. 25-55. Accordingly, the Company is filing supporting schedules as follows:

**Schedule A – Summary of revenue decoupling reconciliation and calculation of the RDAF**

Schedule A consists of nine pages which present the calculation of the proposed RDAF. Page 1 of Schedule A presents a summary of the components of the proposed allocated RDAF, inclusive of the revenue decoupling adjustment, the 2026 Winter Bill Relief Credit reconciliation, and the 2025 $50 Bill Credit reconciliation. Page 2 presents the proposed allocated RDAF applicable to the revenue decoupling adjustment. The revenue requirement is the total allowable revenue decoupling adjustment on Page 5 that is allocated to rate classes using the base distribution revenue allocator per tariff, M.D.P.U. No. 60N. The allocated revenue requirements are divided by the total forecast sales for each rate group in 2026 to arrive at the proposed RDAF. Page 3 presents the proposed rate applicable to the recovery of 2026 Winter Bill Relief Credits in accordance with the Winter Bill Relief Credit Tariff, M.D.P.U. No. 87. The revenue requirement is the Ending Balance from the 2026 Winter Bill Relief Credits reconciliation on Schedule B, Page 3, allocated to residential rate classes based on the percentage of credits received by rate class and the total [PDF p. 7 | printed p. 3] forecast sales for each rate class in 2026. Page 4 presents the proposed rate applicable to the over-collection of funds from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts related to the April 2025 $50 Bill Credits issued to customers in accordance with Governor Healey's Energy Affordability Agenda. The revenue requirement is the ending balance from the April 2025 $50 Bill Credits reconciliation on Schedule B, Page 4, allocated to the residential rate class.

Page 5 of Schedule A presents the summary of the Company's RDAF reconciliation, excluding the 2026 Winter Bill Relief Credits and April 2025 $50 Bill Credits:

| Item                                                      | Amount        |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Revenue Decoupling Adjustment – Jan 2026 through Dec 2026 | $(34,468,668) |
| Adjustment to ATR for Sale of Streetlight Assets          | $(35,395)     |
| Revenue Decoupling Reconciliation (prior period)          | $12,946,758   |
| Total Revenue Decoupling Adjustment                       | $(21,557,305) |
| Excess Revenue Decoupling Adjustment above Cap            | $0            |
| Total Allowable Revenue Decoupling Adjustment             | $(21,557,305) |

The Company tests whether the Total Revenue Decoupling Adjustment ("RDA") (Page 5, Line 11) exceeds the Company's Revenue Decoupling Adjustment Cap. This cap, as set out in Section 1.07 of the RDAM tariff, is one percent of the Company's total revenue. The Company used total revenue from calendar year 2025, with supply revenue imputed at the Basic Service price for customers with competitive suppliers. As shown on lines 13 to 17, the Company's Revenue Decoupling Adjustment does not exceed the one percent cap, resulting in an Allowable Revenue Decoupling Adjustment of ($21,557,305).

Pages 6 through 9 present the calculation of the Adjustment to Annual Target Revenue for the Sale of Streetlight Assets ("Street Light Sales Adjustment"). The Company is including a credit of $35,395 to the revenue decoupling adjustment and reconciling balance as summarized above and shown on Page 5, Line 4. The Street Light Sales Adjustment represents the portion of the Company's streetlight revenue requirement that has been "avoided" due to the sale of its assets.

**Schedules L and M – Heat Pump Reporting**

In compliance with the Orders issued in D.P.U. 25-08 and D.P.U. 25-55 with respect to the residential heat pump rate, the Company provides Schedule L which shows twelve months of pre-enrollment monthly kWh and all available months following enrollment through September 2026 (D.P.U. 25-55 at 27) and Schedule M which shows the calculation of billed base distribution revenue recorded from heat pump customers in comparison to the base distribution revenue that would have been billed if those customers had not enrolled in the heat pump rates (D.P.U. 25-08 at 41).

Schedule L shows the monthly kWh sales for all the customers who have enrolled in the heat pump rate since November 2025 when the heat pump rates became effective. The rate class represents the rate class assigned at the time of the customer's enrollment. The enrollment date represents the first month that the customer was billed on the heat pump rate. The monthly kWh sales are from the billing system and reflect the month the bills were issued. If there were two bills issued in one month, due to the timing of the bills, then there may be no sales in one month and the next month would show the sales for two months. The Company provides sales for all customers from November 2024 through September 2026. November 2024 would be 12 months before the enrollment for customers who enrolled at the start of the rate offering, and September 2026 is the latest bill data available. The sales shown before the customer's enrollment date would have been [PDF p. 10 | printed p. 6] sales billed under the non-heat pump rate. Customers who enrolled in a heat pump rate may have pre-existing heat pump installations in their homes.

Schedule M provides the calculation of the billed base distribution revenue for enrolled heat pump customers under the heat pump rate with the revenues that would have been billed if those customers had not enrolled in the heat pump rate. The revenue calculated is based only on the base distribution rate for the winter period when there is a price difference between the heat pump rate and the non-heat pump rate, which would be comparable to the revenues reported in the annual revenue decoupling filing. Furthermore, the revenue calculation compares the revenue since the customer's enrollment date through April 2026. The underlying usage used for the revenue comparison is the same.

## Lessons Learned, Successes, and Challenges with MEO Efforts

[PDF p. 11 | printed p. 7]

The Company used a variety of marketing, education, and outreach ("MEO") channels to promote the Heat Pump Rate and reach a broad range of customers in Massachusetts, including income-eligible households and customers in Environmental Justice (EJ) communities.

In addition to direct customer outreach for auto-enrolled customers, in November 2025, the Company distributed a bill insert and included an on-bill message for all customers to increase customer awareness of the new Heat Pump Rate. The Company plans to use these communication channels again in November 2026.

The Company also developed a customer-facing flyer, in English and Spanish, that includes information about the Heat Pump Rate on one side and information about current energy efficiency incentives for installing heat pumps on the other. The flyer has been distributed at a variety of customer events. The Company found the flyer to be a useful tool to connect customers with information about both heat pump incentives and the Heat Pump Rate in a single resource. Based on lessons learned from its outreach, the Company will continue to use the flyer in 2026 and into 2027, with one adjustment based on direction from The Department, and is including Portuguese, Haitian Creole and Simplified Chinese iterations. The updated material will clarify that the Heat Pump Rate is a temporary measure prior to the transition to a new rate structure. The company also plans to use the voice of the customer community to test the flyer to see if further adjustments can be made to improve this marketing tool.

As part of its continued outreach efforts, the Company will evaluate opportunities to expand language accessibility for these materials, with consideration given to languages identified in the Department's Language Access Plan.

The Company's website has been a central resource to increase awareness of the Heat Pump Rate. The rate was promoted on the Company's homepage in October 2025, and the Company is using the homepage again in 2026 to prominently promote the Heat Pump Rate in October. Heat Pump Rate information is also incorporated into several relevant areas of the Company's website, including its Rates and Tariffs page, a newly developed and dedicated heat pump rate informational page, a heat pump informational page and a Heat Pumps and Your Bill page. Providing information across multiple relevant webpages allows customers to encounter information about the rate while researching rates, heat pump technology, energy efficiency incentives or the potential impact of a heat pump on their electric bill. Customers may also reach the heat pump rate and heat pump informational pages directly from QR codes or vanity URLs in print outreach material. In 2026-2027, the company also plans to implement a multi-lingual website strategy to improve access to information for customers with limited English proficiency, with consideration given to the languages identified in the Department's Language Access Plan (LAP) and the language needs of customers with limited English proficiency.

A key adjustment to the Company's 2026-2027 MEO strategy is additional outreach specifically intended to reach income-eligible households. The Company added information about the Heat [PDF p. 12 | printed p. 8] Pump Rate to its Programs to Help one-pager. This resource provides information about programs available to income-eligible customers and is posted on the Company's website and distributed throughout the service territory through Community Action Agencies and at customer events. Including the Heat Pump Rate in this resource provides an additional opportunity to reach income-eligible customers through established assistance-focused communications and community channels.

Overall, the Company's experience has reinforced the importance of using multiple communication channels and integrating Heat Pump Rate information into existing customer touchpoints. A continuing challenge is ensuring customers receive sufficient context to understand the Heat Pump Rate, including its temporary nature and its relationship to future rate structures. The Company's 2026-2027 adjustments address these lessons by clarifying the temporary nature of the rate on customer-facing materials, incorporating Heat Pump Rate information into the Programs to Help resource to expand outreach to income-eligible households, and improving language accessibility through its planned multilingual website strategy, particularly for income-eligible customers and EJ populations.

With these adjustments, the Company plans to continue its multi-channel MEO approach and does not recommend additional changes to the marketing strategy at this time.

## Collaborative Efforts with Stakeholders

Eversource works with community-based organizations and other stakeholders, including organizations serving EJ communities, to share heat pump information through Let's Talk Energy workshops and bill assistance events. These partnerships also provide opportunities to increase awareness of seasonal heat pump rate and identify questions or potential barriers to customer understanding, particularly among income-eligible households and EJ populations. The Company plans to continue these collaborations to support customer education and outreach.
