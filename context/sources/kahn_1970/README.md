## Structure

The book is organized into two parts:

- **Part I: The Institution of Regulated Monopoly** (Chapters 1–2) — Establishes the rationale for regulation and surveys traditional regulatory practice: rate base determination, rate of return, cost of service.
- **Part II: Economic Principles of Rate Making** (Chapters 3–7) — Develops the microeconomic theory of efficient pricing for regulated industries: marginal cost pricing, peak-load pricing, price discrimination under decreasing costs, competitive rate making, and the practical qualifications to these principles.

- **Italics:** Restored for book titles, case names (_Munn v. Illinois_, _Hope Natural Gas_), Latin phrases (_op. cit._, _passim_), and emphasis, based on context.
- **Mathematical notation:** Economic variables (LRMC, SRMC, ATC, MC) and subscripted terms (D₁, P₂, Q₃) are preserved.
