_Extractor notes (not source text):_

- **Page markers.** Every PDF page is either marked `[PDF p. N | printed p. M]` or sits inside a `[PDF pp. A-B not transcribed: …]` run. Every page prints the exhibit stamp "Exhibit CLP-RATES-2 / Page N of 209" in its header, which equals the PDF page number, so `printed p.` repeats the PDF page; cite that page ("Exhibit CLP-RATES-2 at 45"). Each sheet also prints its own label ("EXHIBIT CLP-RATES-2.4 … Page 1 of 75"); it is transcribed at the top of each page as source text, not used in the marker.
- **Line numbers.** Most sheets print spreadsheet row numbers down the left margin, so the citation scheme is `page_line`. A `{n}` prefix is a printed row number; `{a-b}` before a table means the table occupies rows a to b. On some pages the first rows (1-9) or the later rows (10 onward, on PDF pp. 181 and 183) are printed in a way the text layer does not read as margin numbers; those rows carry no prefix. Running headers (company name, docket, exhibit stamp) are left out.
- **Numbers.** The PDF prints most figures rounded to whole units (`$000` or MWh). An earlier version of this extract was read from the Excel workbook (`openpyxl`, cached values), and where it gave unrounded workbook values (for example `8,716,958.14` MWh), the tables keep them; this repair checked every one of them against the PDF and each rounds to the printed figure, except where a `PDF-vs-workbook` note says otherwise. Rows, columns and tables added in this repair are as printed. Dollar figures are `$(000)'s` unless noted; per-unit rates are `$` per bill or `$` per kWh.
- **Scope.** Residential classes only (Rate 1 — Residential Electric Service, Rate 5 — Residential Electric Heating, Rate 7 — Residential Time-of-Day), plus the company totals the residential figures are compared against. Rate 6 (new optional residential space heating rate) has no workpaper in Exhibit CLP-RATES-2.4, because it is a new rate with no billing history; its tariff is in `exhibit_clp_rates_3_residential_tariffs.md`.
- **Exhibit CLP-RATES-2.10 (section 4).** Total Revenue there is all-in (distribution, transmission, public benefits and supply) at current rates, not the base delivery revenue requirement; for Rate 1's base delivery revenue use the Distribution functional category in section 3a. Its bills and sales columns are the sources for customer counts and kWh. "Average Number of Customer Bills Rendered" is an average monthly count (annual bills = 12 ×).
- **Corrections to the earlier version.** The earlier version (a) had no page markers or front matter; (b) omitted Exhibit CLP-RATES-2.12 (decoupling targets, PDF p. 181), now transcribed; (c) mislabeled several Exhibit CLP-RATES-2.1 columns (column I is "Distribution Target Revenue", not "Proposed Rate Target Revenue"; allocations print as `102%` and `120%`, not `1.02` and `1.20`); (d) gave Rate 1's Test Year normalized Generation Services revenue as `1,079,662.25`, where the PDF prints `1,079,661` (see section 3a); (e) described the workbook's Rate 5 Rate Year bill-count cell as corrupted, where the PDF prints the same text ("EXHIBIT CLP-R") and the printed company bill total leaves Rate 5 out (section 4b); (f) said the Rate 1 percent change is computed twice in the workpaper (`18.075%` vs `18.079%`), which the PDF does not show: each tab prints one rounded figure, `18.1%`.
