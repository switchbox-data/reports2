**Request.** According to Ameren witness Eric Sackett, the "budget associated with make ready projects are classified as a Category 2 investment within the 2028 Grid Plan." (Ameren Ex. 15.0, 16.) Please provide the projected level of investment associated with utility-side make-ready infrastructure supporting electric vehicle charging for each year of the 2029-2031 Grid Plan period.

**RESPONSE**

**Prepared By:** Eric Sackett\
**Title:** Senior Manager – Beneficial Electrification\
**Phone No.:** 1 447-287-7713

Ameren Illinois objects to this request to the extent it seeks new studies or analyses not previously prepared, kept, or maintained in the ordinary course of business and to the extent it calls for speculation. Subject to and without waiving these objections, Ameren Illinois responds as follows:

Category 2 New Business investments are not separated by project type. These investments represent a blanket allocation that covers all new business projects across Ameren Illinois' service territory. There is no separately allocated specific budget for utility-side make-ready infrastructure related to EV charging projects within the 2029-2031 Grid Plan period. Instead, these projects are treated as standard new business investments and therefore utilize Ameren's standard line extension policy. Historically, under BE Plan 1, EV infrastructure projects experienced a reduction of $1.1 million in upfront utility-side costs through the revenue test. This historical figure is a reasonable expectation of future EV-related infrastructure projects through Category 2 New Business investments.
