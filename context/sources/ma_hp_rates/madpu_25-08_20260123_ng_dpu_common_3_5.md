Information Request DPU-Common-3-5-NG

**Request:**

Refer to Petition, Exhibit D. Please explain whether the Company collects the data needed to report on the percentage of customers eligible for the seasonal heat pump rate who are enrolled in it. As part of this response, describe any costs, operational challenges, or other barriers associated with tracking and reporting this data.

**Response:**

The Company has put significant effort into enrolling all customers it knows to be eligible for the heat pump rate. Before the launch of the rate on November 1, 2025, the Company automatically enrolled a significant majority of customers who had received a heat pump from a Mass Save® sponsor after January 1, 2019. Subsequent to the launch of the rate, the Company has been automatically enrolling all new customers receiving Mass Save rebates for heat pumps on a weekly basis. The Mass Save program drives a very large number of the heat pump installations in the Commonwealth, including through Mass Save's programs that target multiple different types of customer groups.

However, the Company has no means of collecting data on how many additional customers in the Commonwealth have an eligible heat pump but have not yet enrolled, and therefore the Company cannot report such a metric.
