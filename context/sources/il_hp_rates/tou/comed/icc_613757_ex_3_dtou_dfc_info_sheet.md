**NOTES:**

(1) This informational sheet is supplemental to Rate BESH - Basic Electric Service Hourly Pricing (Rate BESH) and Rate RDS - Retail Delivery Service (Rate RDS).

<!-- EPON: midday-peak,bundling — tariff definition of the DTOU periods in Central Prevailing Time, applying EVERY day (no weekday/weekend or summer/non-summer distinction): Mid-Day 1:00–7:00 p.m., Morning 6:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m., Evening 7:00–11:00 p.m., Overnight 11:00 p.m.–6:00 a.m.; sheet is a supplement to the time-variant supply/hourly rate tariffs (BESH, RDS) -->

(2) The Time of Use Distribution Facilities Charges apply to electricity delivered every day during the following Central Prevailing Time (CPT) periods: the Mid-Day Period from 1:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., the Morning Period from 6:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., the Evening Period from 7:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m., and the Overnight Period from 11:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m.

(3) The DFCs shown on retail customer bills include various adjustments:

$$
\mathrm{DFC\ \&\ ADJ} = \mathrm{DFC} \times (\mathrm{IDUF} + \mathrm{DRAF} + \mathrm{EDAF} + \mathrm{TPAF} + \mathrm{RBAF}_D) + \mathrm{DGRA}_D
$$

Where:

- **IDUF** = The incremental distribution uncollectible cost factor applicable for residential retail customers (IDUF<sub>R</sub>) equals the applicable IDUF<sub>R</sub> listed in Informational Sheet No. 20; the incremental distribution uncollectible cost factor applicable for nonresidential retail customers to which the Small Load delivery class is applicable (IDUF<sub>N</sub>) equals the applicable IDUF<sub>N</sub> listed in Informational Sheet No. 20; and
- **DRAF** = The applicable Delivery Reconciliation Adjustment Factor listed in Informational Sheet No. 9.
- **EDAF** = The applicable Excess Deferred Income Tax Factor listed in Informational Sheet No. 62.
- **TPAF** = The applicable Total Plan Adjustment Factor listed in Informational Sheet No. 65.
- **RBAF<sub>D</sub>** = The applicable Revenue Balancing Adjustment Factor for a Delivery Class D listed in Informational Sheet No. 18.
- **DGRA<sub>D</sub>** = The applicable Distributed Generation (DG) Rebate Adjustment listed in Informational Sheet No. 56.

(4) These Time of Use DSCs [sic; "DSCs" as in source, likely meaning DFCs] must be updated in the event that the Company updates DFCs in the Delivery Service Charges informational sheets.
