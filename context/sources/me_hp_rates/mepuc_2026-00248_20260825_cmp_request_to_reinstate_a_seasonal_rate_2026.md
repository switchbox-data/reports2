## OVERVIEW OF A-SEASONAL (HEAT PUMP) RATE

On September 27, 2022, the Commission issued an Order Approving the Stipulation in Docket No. 2021-00325, dated September 22, 2022. As part of the Order, in accordance with Paragraph 9 of the Revised Stipulation, CMP worked with other Stipulating Parties to develop the proposed Rate A-Seasonal rate, available to customers who verbally affirmed heat pump ownership at their service location. All transmission and distribution kWh charges associated with the annual revenue requirement would be collected during the six-month period from May through October, with no transmission or distribution kWh charges assessed during the six-month period from November through April. All other rate components that include assessments for the Electric Lifeline Program, Efficiency Maine Trust and Stranded Costs would be recovered on a year-round basis. The rate was intended to enable the purchase and use of heat pumps in an effort to support State policies regarding climate change and electrification.

On October 26, 2022, the Commission approved CMP's proposed A-Seasonal Rate as a pilot program. CMP implemented the new rate on January 1, 2023, designating a pilot period termination date of October 31, 2024.

On December 1, 2023, as part of the Follow-on Rate Design Proceeding, Docket No. 2022-00152, the Company proposed extending the termination date for the A-Seasonal Rate to January 31, 2026. The Company made this proposal based on collaborative meetings with, and

<!-- PDF page 2 (printed "Page | 2") -->

input from, interested parties and stakeholders in the proceeding. The Commission approved the extension of the termination date on March 1, 2024.[^1]

On January 29, 2026, CMP filed a cancellation of the A-Seasonal Rate in compliance with the Termination Date language, effective February 1, 2026. Prior to filing the cancellation, CMP consulted with interested parties and stakeholders to assess support for extending the expiration date. At that time, residential heating fuel prices were stable, and CMP did not receive significant support for an extension. Accordingly, CMP did not seek to extend the A-Seasonal Rate.

## PROPOSAL TO REINSTATE A-SEASONAL RATE

CMP respectfully requests that the Commission approve the reinstatement of the A-Seasonal Rate. The Company makes this request as part of its ongoing efforts to implement optional delivery rate designs that provide customers with choices and meaningful benefits, while keeping affordability in mind. Interested parties and stakeholders have approached CMP requesting reinstatement of the A-Seasonal Rate to help mitigate the increased costs customers are experiencing as a result of recent significant increases in heating fuel prices. CMP agrees with the interested parties and stakeholders that reinstatement of the A-Seasonal Rate could assist customers in managing and lowering their overall heating costs. Additionally, given the current pricing for heating fuel, reinstating the A-Seasonal Rate may encourage wider adoption of heat pumps.

The Company proposes limited changes to the A-Seasonal Rate language as it existed prior to February 2026. CMP recommends removing the termination date, with all other rate provisions remaining unchanged, as shown in the rate schedule attachment. Pricing is based on the Company's most recent July 1, 2026, price change (please see Attachment 1).

CMP looks forward to working further with the Commission on the reinstatement of the A-Seasonal Rate. Please contact me with any questions or if additional information is required regarding this filing.
