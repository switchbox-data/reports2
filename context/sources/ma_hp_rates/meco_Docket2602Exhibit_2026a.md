# Docket 26-02: Exhibit PP-9, Net Transmission Charge

Exhibit PP-9 of National Grid's annual retail rate filing in D.P.U. 26-02, calculating the net transmission charge effective March 1, 2026. Adds the company-wide $44.4M under-recovery to the 2026 transmission forecast and spreads the total across R-1/R-2, G-1, G-2, G-3 and streetlights by coincident-peak share (R-1/R-2 53.6%). Read for who absorbs a shortfall.

- **A** (62 rows x 11 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **B** (35 rows x 12 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Forecast** (50 rows x 11 columns): FORECAST
