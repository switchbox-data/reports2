# CMP Electric Technology (ET) Rate — Tariff Details

**Source:** [CMP ET Rate Page](https://www.cmpco.com/w/new-electric-technology-rate) and [CMP Pricing Page](https://www.cmpco.com/account/understandyourbill/pricing)
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2026
**Docket No.:** 2022-00152

## Basic Rate Per Month

- Service charge: $41.24/mo (does NOT include first 50 kWh like Rate A)
- Per kWh charge: $0.088638/kWh (all kWh)
- First 750 kWh of usage is sales-tax free
- Transmission component: $0.047973/kWh (same as Rate A, per Section 44.1)

## Eligibility & Status

- Available to all residential customers (no heat pump or EV requirement)
- Breakeven vs. Rate A at approximately 642 kWh/month
- Best for customers using >800 kWh/month
- Currently set to expire January 2027; CMP has requested MPUC to make it permanent

## Historical Rates

At launch (January 1, 2023):

- Service charge: $31.67/mo
- Per kWh charge: $0.052507/kWh

## Comparison to Rate A (July 2026)

| Component        | Rate A         | ET Rate      | Difference                             |
| ---------------- | -------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Service charge   | $27.37/mo      | $41.24/mo    | +$13.87/mo                             |
| Per kWh delivery | $0.119590      | $0.088638    | -$0.030952 (-25.9%)                    |
| First 50 kWh     | Included in FC | Not included | ET customers pay per-kWh for all usage |

## Design Logic

The ET rate shifts revenue recovery from volumetric charges to the fixed charge. The higher fixed charge ($41.24 vs. $27.37) collects more revenue regardless of usage, allowing a lower per-kWh rate. This benefits high-usage customers (heat pumps, EVs) while maintaining revenue neutrality at the class level.

The ET rate does NOT vary by season — it is the same per-kWh rate year-round. This contrasts with the cancelled Seasonal Heat Pump (SHP) rate, which had very low winter and very high summer volumetric rates.
