# Docket 23-150: Exhibit HP-3, Heat Pump Rate Calculations and Bill Impacts

Exhibit HP-3 of National Grid's December 30, 2024 compliance filing in D.P.U. 23-150, the first version of the rate calculations the DPU's September 30, 2024 order required. The same five sheets as the January 10, 2025 revision, with an October to March winter period and the same $0.02337 winter charge. Kept to show the revision changed only the winter months and billing units, not the rate.

## Sheets

- **R-1_R-2_Heat-Pump Rate** (90 rows x 18 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **R-1 Factor & Bill Impacts** (42 rows x 7 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **R-2 Factor & Bill Impacts** (43 rows x 7 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Resi Typical Bill (3 Ton HP)** (67 rows x 9 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Resi Typical Bill (5 Ton HP)** (67 rows x 9 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
