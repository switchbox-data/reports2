Information Request DPU-Common-2-3-NG

**Request:**

Refer to the Companies' Joint Comments at 4-5. Please:

(a) explain whether and how the Company will customize marketing, education, and outreach ("MEO") messaging, communication channels, or calls to action to differentiate between customers with a heat pump rebate on record and those who do not; and

(b) discuss any barriers that may exist to developing targeted MEO strategies for these distinct customer segments.

**Response:**

(a) The Company can deliver targeted communications to customers who are not currently enrolled in the Heat Pump Rate, emphasizing the potential savings available through participation.

(b) A key challenge is identifying which customers have a heat pump installed in their home. However, by combining internal customer data with information from the Mass Save programs, the Company can develop a list of potentially eligible but unenrolled customers. This targeted list can be used to encourage enrollment in the Heat Pump Rate, helping customers lower their heating costs.
