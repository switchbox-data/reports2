**Request No. DPU-Common 3-5:**

Refer to Petition, Exhibit D. Please explain whether the Company collects the data needed to report on the percentage of customers eligible for the seasonal heat pump rate who are enrolled in it. As part of this response, describe any costs, operational challenges, or other barriers associated with tracking and reporting this data.

**Response:**

The Company is auto-enrolling on the heat pump rate all customers in its territory who receive a rebate for a heat pump through the MassSave® programs.

However, customers may also install heat pump technology without participating in the energy efficiency programs. Those customers can be enrolled on the seasonal heat pump rate by contacting the Company, affirming that they are (or will) utilize their heat pump during the winter months, and opt in to the seasonal heat pump rate. While the Company anticipates that the vast majority of electric customers who utilize a heat pump for heating will be enrolled on the heat pump rate either automatically or by opting in, the Company will not be able to identify customers who adopted a heat pump outside of the MassSave energy efficiency programs who did not opt into the seasonal heat pump rate. Therefore, the Company is unable to report on the percentage of customers eligible for the heat pump rate who are enrolled in it.
