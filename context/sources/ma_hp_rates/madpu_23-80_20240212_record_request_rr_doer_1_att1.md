# Docket 23-80: RR-DOER-01 Attachment 1, Heat Pump Rate Design with Winter Transmission

Attachment 1 to Unitil's Feb 12, 2024 response to Record Request RR-DOER-01, which DOER made at the Feb 5, 2024 hearing (Tr. 207-211) after Unitil said it lacked heat pump peak-demand data. One sheet reruns the Exhibit Unitil-JDT-6 model with winter and summer transmission rates and a monthly revenue-neutrality proof. Keep it as the first worked example of applying the usage-proxy method to transmission.

## Sheets

- **Heat Pump Rate w Transmission** (64 rows x 29 columns): Fitchburg Gas and Electric Light Company
