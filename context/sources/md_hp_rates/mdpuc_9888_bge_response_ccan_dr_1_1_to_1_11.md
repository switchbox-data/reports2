**Baltimore Gas and Electric Co.**
**Response to Chesapeake Climate Action Network Data Request 1**
**Request Received: August 21, 2026**
**Response Date: September 08, 2026**
**Sponsor(s): Jason M.B. Manuel**

**Item No.: CCANDR01-05**

For the test year provided by the Company in this rate case, please provide:

a. the Company's calculation of the cost of service (i.e., cost to serve) attributable to residential electric customers with a heat pump as their primary heating source, in the aggregate.
b. a detailed description of the methodology used to develop this cost of service figure.
c. data sources, allocators, and assumptions used, in live Microsoft Excel format with all formulae unlocked.
i. If the Company's cost of service calculations use a different period than the one specified above, please identify the period actually used.
ii. If the Company does not possess the information necessary to calculate the cost of service for heat pump customers, please explain in detail why not.
iii. If the Company is unable to produce this figure for the test year, but is able to do so for a different time period, please use the same time period used to satisfy Requests 3 and 4.

**RESPONSE:**

As described in the Company's response to CCANDR01-01, the Company assigns subclass designations for heating and non-heating loads based on available information provided by the customer at the time the account is established and is primarily used for Company internal purposes to assist with load forecasting. The Company does not calculate the cost to serve or perform a cost of service study for a residential customer by heat source and does not have an established methodology for allocating distribution costs among customers based on heating source. The Company has filed in this proceeding an electric cost of service study ("ECOSS") as discussed in the Direct Testimony of Company Witness Manuel. The ECOSS provides the cost to serve different customer classes by FERC account, as enunciated in the FERC Uniform System of Accounts. The Company's ECOSS is designed to allocate costs based on cost-causation, using various classifications and allocations based on demand, energy usage, customer counts, etc. A customer's heating source is not generally used as a cost allocation factor in the Company's ECOSS because heat source on its own does not determine the distribution facilities that are required to serve that customer. The ECOSS includes the residential customer class (Schedules R, RD, and RL). Please refer to StaffDR05-01 Attachment 1 for a working Excel model of the Company's ECOSS.

A gas cost of service study has not been filed in this proceeding as this is an electric distribution-only base rate case.

For the same period, resolution, timestamp convention, and machine-readable Microsoft Excel or CSV format specified in Request 8, please provide the same aggregate electric energy usage, broken out separately by rate schedule/customer class (e.g., Schedules R, RL, RD, G, GP, GS, GU, GL, T, PL, EVP, and any other applicable rate schedule), with each rate schedule's interval data aligned to the same timestamps used in the preceding request so that the class-level results can be summed interval-by-interval. If the Company does not possess the information needed to produce the load profile for any rate schedule, please state specifically which schedule(s) the Company is unable to provide, and explain in detail why not for each.

**RESPONSE:**

The Company has not performed this analysis. Demand data by class for each calendar month of 2025 and 2026 is not currently available. Preparation of study-quality load data requires multiple processing and validation steps, including extraction of AMI interval data, data validation and quality assurance, reconciliation to billing determinants, application of voltage level line loss factors, and reconciliation and scaling to PJM System Sendout. These processes are performed exclusively for the development of cost of service studies to be submitted in distribution rate cases, with 2024 being the most recent calendar year for which these processes were performed.

For data currently available that most closely matches the request, please refer to _CCANDR01-11 Attachment 1_, which provides the requested information for calendar year 2024.
