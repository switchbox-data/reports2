# Heat Pump Installations

Eversource Connecticut publishes this export from the Connecticut Energy Efficiency Dashboard for its Conservation and Load Management programs. The sheet counts residential heat pump installations for Eversource Electric by year and equipment type from 2017 through 2026. Cited in CIEC-1 because 21,765 ducted and ground-source installations sit between the Company's enrollment floor and the 9% ceiling, and the total since 2023 far exceeds the 4,400 full-displacement rebates.
