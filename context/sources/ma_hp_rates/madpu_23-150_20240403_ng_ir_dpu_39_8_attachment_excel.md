# Docket 23-150: Attachment DPU-39-8, Electrification Pricing Bill Impacts (Rate R-1)

National Grid's April 3, 2024 answer to the DPU's information request 39-8 in D.P.U. 23-150, which asked for a version of Attachment DPU-26-1 (not in our files) limited to R-1. Two sheets of live formulas give the R-1 Electrification Pricing bill impact and revenue shift, and R-1 bill frequency counts by usage band. The first bill-frequency revenue-shift model, superseded by Attachment RR-DPU-42.

## Sheets

- **Bill Impacts Calculation R1** (41 rows x 7 columns): Inputs
- **Bill Frequency Data R1** (55 rows x 10 columns): USAGE_GROUP
  (kWh), Range Min, Range Max, </> Average Usage, NUM_ACCTS, PERCENT OF TOTAL, Cumulative Percent, Average Usage By Group (kWh), Percent of High Usage, Weighted Consumption by Frequency in High Consumption Group
