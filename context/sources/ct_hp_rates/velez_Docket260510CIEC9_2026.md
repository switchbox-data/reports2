# Docket 26-05-10: CIEC-9(c) Workpaper, Winter-Peaking Marginal Cost

Workpaper supporting CLF-SC's response to CIEC-9(c) in the Eversource Connecticut rate case. Gives the average converting home's hourly load before and after the heat pump and the testimony's hourly marginal capacity costs, with an illustrative winter-peaking incremental marginal cost as formulas. Read for how much heat pump customers' marginal cost could rise if the system became winter peaking.

## Sheets

- **README** (15 rows x 1 columns): CIEC-9(c) workpaper
- **hourly** (8761 rows x 14 columns): timestamp, season, kwh_before, kwh_after, kwh_added, mc_dist, mc_tx, rank_in_season, mc_dist_reassigned, mc_tx_reassigned
