# Docket 26-05-10: JPV-4, Rate HP Derivation

Workbook exhibit filed with Velez Direct in the Eversource Connecticut rate case. Derives Rate HP's seasonal distribution and transmission rates from the average fossil fuel-heated home's monthly usage, Rate 1's rates and the incremental marginal cost, as formulas. Read for how the 71% and 75% winter rate reductions were calculated.

## Sheets

- **monthly** (16 rows x 13 columns): month, season, kwh_pre_retrofit, kwh_post_retrofit, rate1_distribution_energy_$/kwh, rate1_riders_$/kwh, rate1_transmission_$/kwh, rate1_generation_$/kwh, distribution_incl_riders_pre_$, transmission_pre_$, distribution_incl_riders_post_$, transmission_post_$, ...
- **marginal_cost** (5 rows x 4 columns): Annual marginal T&D cost of the representative home (hourly_marginal_cost sheet of JPV3-representative_home_load_profiles.xlsx)
- **rate_hp** (23 rows x 5 columns): Rate HP winter rates: remove the Rate 1 overpayment, keep summer charges at Rate 1
