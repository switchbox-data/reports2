# Docket 26-05-10: CIEC-2(d) Workpaper, RDM and TAC Non-Residential Impact

Workpaper supporting CLF-SC's response to CIEC-2(d) in the Eversource Connecticut rate case. Contains the inputs (per-customer revenue decrease, existing customer count, RDM and TAC allocation shares) and formulas for the annual distribution and transmission shortfall from existing-customer auto-enrollment, including the non-residential share and the RDM ¢/kWh impact. Read for the reconciling-mechanism calculations in the response.
