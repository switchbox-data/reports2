# TERMS AND CONDITIONS

**ELIGIBILITY:** Incentives are available to Eversource or United Illuminating (UI) (Companies) residential electric service customers (Customer). **Equipment must be installed by a contractor participating in the Energize Connecticut Heat Pump Installer Network.** Customers must replace existing oil, propane, natural gas or electric baseboard heating systems as the primary heating source to qualify. If an existing natural gas, oil or propane system will be left in place, an integrated control must be installed. Eligible homes include single families and 1–4 unit multifamily structures. Please be sure to verify your model at: **EnergizeCT.com/HPQPL**

**APPLICATION OFFER:** This Air Source Heat Pump Energy Optimization Rebate program (Program) covers products purchased on or after January 1, 2023 and installed by December 31, 2023 to be eligible for the rebate. Details of this Program, including rebate levels, are subject to change or cancellation without prior notice. This Application along with required documentation must be postmarked by March 31, 2024. Funding for this rebate Program is limited to the period indicated or while funds last. Air source heat pump system capacity is the AHRI (Air-Conditioning, Heating and Refrigeration Institute) rated cooling tonnage. This rebate may not be combined with any other utility mail-in rebate offer for the same equipment.

**PROOF OF PURCHASE:** The following documentation must accompany each rebate Application: An installation service invoice itemizing the purchased System indicating the installer's name and address, the System type, manufacturer/brand and model numbers, the installed date and address, the total installation cost and the date of purchase.

**EQUIPMENT:** Air source heat pumps must be listed on the air source heat pump qualified product list: **EnergizeCT.com/HPQPL**. Integrated controls must be listed on the integrated control qualified product list: **EnergizeCT.com/ICQPL**

**APPLICATION:** This Application must be filled out completely, truthfully and accurately. The Customer and contractor (if applicable) must each sign the completed Application and it must be submitted along with the PROOF OF PURCHASE requirements listed above.

**PAYMENT:** Please allow up to 90 days for payment. Payment processing may take longer if information is missing on the Application. Please call EFI at **1-877-364-4217** if you have any questions about your rebate incentive.

**APPROVAL AND VERIFICATION:** The Companies reserve the right to verify sales transaction and to have reasonable access to your residence to inspect the system installed under this Program prior to issuing incentives for up to one year after date of Application.
