# Docket 26-02: Exhibit PP-8, Transmission Reconciliation

Exhibit PP-8 of National Grid's annual retail rate filing in D.P.U. 26-02 (docket opened Jan. 15, 2026), reconciling its base transmission revenue against expense for Oct. 2024 to Sept. 2025 as one company-wide balance, an under-recovery of $44.4M, with no class split. Read for how National Grid carries a transmission shortfall before it is allocated to classes.

## Sheets

- **Transmission P1** (204 rows x 11 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Transmission P2** (178 rows x 19 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Transmission P3** (172 rows x 11 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Transmission P4** (173 rows x 11 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **MECO input** (54 rows x 18 columns): Transmission Service Cost (SH) + Coincident Peak, Transmission Adjustment Factor (6Y), MECO Transmission Expense
- **NANT input** (52 rows x 14 columns): Transmission Service Cost (SH), Transmission Adjustment Factor (6Y), NANT Transmission Expense
