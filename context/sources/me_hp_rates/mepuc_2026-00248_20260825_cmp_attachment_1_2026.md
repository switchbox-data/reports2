# Docket 2026-00248: Attachment 1, Proposed Price Change and Rate Design for Rate A-Seasonal

CMP's Attachment 1 to its Aug. 25, 2026 request to reinstate the A-Seasonal heat pump rate (Docket 2026-00248), pricing it at July 1, 2026 rates for an Oct. 1 start. Four sheets: price summary, component rate design, May-October heat pump transmission rate, and monthly bills for a modeled heat pump home. We keep it as the only worked derivation of the rate.

## Sheets

- **Att 1 Summary** (28 rows x 9 columns): Central Maine Power Company
- **Att 1 Rate Design p2** (74 rows x 19 columns): Heat Pump Rate
- **Att 1 Rate Design p3** (32 rows x 34 columns): Central Maine Power Company
- **Att 1 Rate Design p4** (222 rows x 17 columns): Heat Pump Customer
