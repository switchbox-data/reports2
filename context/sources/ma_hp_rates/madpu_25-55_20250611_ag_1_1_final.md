**Information Request:**

Please indicate whether the Company has collected and/or analyzed the actual monthly kwh usage of its residential customers with heat pumps. If so, please provide the analyses and all results that came from that collection and/or analyses.

**Response:**

The Company has not analyzed the actual monthly kWh usage of its residential customers with heat pumps. However, this type of analysis has been performed by Guidehouse Inc. for the electric and gas program administrators. Please see Tables 6-1 and 6-2 of Attachment AG-1-1.
