# Docket 23-150: Attachment RR-DPU-42, Electrification Pricing Bill Impacts and Bill Frequency Data

National Grid's June 5, 2024 answer to record request RR-DPU-42 from the May 29, 2024 hearings in D.P.U. 23-150, asking it to correct Attachment DPU-39-8 and rerun the Electrification Pricing revenue-shift analysis for R-2. Four sheets of live formulas give R-1 and R-2 bill impacts and the shift under opt-in scenarios, plus billing-frequency counts. Sizes an opt-in rate's cost shift without a heat pump customer list; supersedes DPU-39-8.

## Sheets

- **Bill Impacts Calculation R1** (41 rows x 7 columns): Inputs
- **Bill Impacts Calculation R2** (41 rows x 7 columns): Inputs
- **Bill Frequency Data R1** (55 rows x 10 columns): USAGE_GROUP
  (kWh), Range Min, Range Max, </> Average Usage, NUM_ACCTS, PERCENT OF TOTAL, Cumulative Percent, Average Usage By Group (kWh), Percent of High Usage, Weighted Consumption by Frequency in High Consumption Group
- **Bill Frequency Data R2** (55 rows x 10 columns): USAGE_GROUP
  (kWh), Range Min, Range Max, </> Average Usage, NUM_ACCTS, PERCENT OF TOTAL, Cumulative Percent, Average Usage By Group (kWh), Percent of High Usage, Weighted Consumption by Frequency in High Consumption Group
