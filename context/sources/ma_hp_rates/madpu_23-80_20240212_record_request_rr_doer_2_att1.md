# Docket 23-80: RR-DOER-02 Attachment 1, Heat Pump Rate Design with All Riders

Attachment 1 to Unitil's Feb 12, 2024 response to Record Request RR-DOER-02, which DOER made at the Feb 5, 2024 hearing to extend the winter heat pump rate calculation to all reconciling mechanisms. One sheet recombines six riders into a winter rate of $0.0449 per kWh against $0.12577 in summer, with a revenue-neutrality proof. Keep it to show the revenue-neutral method applied to every per-kWh charge.
