# Docket 25-55: Attachment DOER-1-2(b), Heat Pump Rate Design with Corrected Summer Period

Second workbook attached to Eversource's 6/11/25 response to DOER 1-2 in D.P.U. 25-55: the originally proposed ES-HP-2 model with the Sch. 3 bill impact summer corrected from May-October, which the company said it had used inadvertently, to June-September. Rates are unchanged, so only bill impacts move. We keep it to pair with the (a) workbook and the original as the company's own correction of its bill impact numbers.
