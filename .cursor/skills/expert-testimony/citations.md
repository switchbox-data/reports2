# Citation conventions for testimony

These are the conventions our paralegal applied to the filed version of Switchbox's direct testimony in Maryland PSC Case No. 9888 (`context/sources/md_hp_rates/mdpuc_9888_direct_testimony_velez.md`, footnotes 1–71). Use them for every citation in every testimony, in every docket, unless the commission's rules require otherwise. Where the filed testimony was inconsistent, the rule below takes the form it used most often; those cases are marked _(standardized)_.

The examples are real citations. Copy their punctuation exactly: commas, the word "at", en dashes in ranges, and where the parentheses go.

## Pinpoints

- **Testimony and transcripts: `at page:lines`.** `Karas Direct at 4:13–14.` A range that crosses pages: `at 21:11–22:7`. Separate spans: `at 8:18–22, 9:1–3`. A whole page: `at 22`.
- **Everything else: `at page`.** `Order No. 91461, at 15`, `at 13`, `at 291`. Several pages: `at 15–16`.
- **Get every pinpoint from `just sources-cite`.** It prints, e.g., `14:5-7 (PDF p. 17)`; you write `at 14:5–7`, with an en dash. Never count pages or lines yourself (see "Citations" in `SKILL.md` for repairing extracts without markers).
- **Never write "PDF p."** in testimony. When a page prints no number, cite the smallest unit the document labels instead: the sheet (`at Sheet E-13`), the attachment (`Attach. 1`), or the section of an order or plan (`at Section C.2.b`).
- **Never write "p.", "pp.", "line" or "lines"** in a pinpoint.

## Witness testimony

**First citation** names the testimony type, the witness and the party, defines the short form, and gives the date:

> Direct Testimony of Jennifer A. Karas on behalf of Baltimore Gas and Electric Company ("Karas Direct"), at 4:13–14 (July 2, 2026).

- Use the testimony type as the cover prints it, minus "Prepared" or "Pre-filed": `Direct Testimony`, `Rebuttal Testimony`, `Surrebuttal Testimony`.
- The short form is the witness's surname plus the type: `Karas Direct`, `Karas Rebuttal`, `Velez Direct`.
- The date is the filing date, in parentheses at the end, after the pinpoint.

**Later citations** use the short form: `Karas Direct at 8:13–14.`

Where the commission numbers each testimony as an exhibit (the Illinois Commerce Commission, for example), put the exhibit number after the short form: `Eidukas Rebuttal, NS-PGL Ex. 12.0 at 16:3–5.`

**Witnesses in another docket** start with the case and its italic caption:

> Case No. 9692, _Baltimore Gas and Electric Company's Application for an Electric and Gas Multi-Year Plan_, Direct Testimony of Mark D. Case ("Case Direct"), Ex. MDC-2.

Later: `Case Direct at 138.` or `Case Direct at Ex. MDC-2, Appendix A: Program Level Modeling Assumptions at 43.`

**Your own earlier testimony** in the same docket follows the same rules: first citation in full, then `Velez Direct at 7:9–12.`

## Exhibits and data responses

- **Your own exhibits:** `Ex. JPV-2`. When the exhibit is a data response, name it: `Ex. JPV-2, BGE Response to CCAN DR 1-4`. With an attachment: `Ex. JPV-3, BGE Response to CCAN DR 1-11, Attach. 1`. In body text, write `Ex. JPV-1` too.
- **Another party's exhibits:** the witness's short form, then the exhibit's label as that party prints it: `Karas Direct, Company Exhibit JAK-2, at Sheet E-13`. _(standardized: the filed testimony also once wrote `Karas Direct, Exhibit JAK-1`.)_
- **A data response attached to another party's exhibit:** `Karas Rebuttal, Company Exhibit JAK-5, BGE Response to OPC DR 7-11, Attach. 1`.
- **A data response that is not an exhibit:** `BGE Response to CCAN DR 1-1`. The form is always `[Responding party] Response to [Requesting party] DR [set]-[question]`, with no leading zeros (`DR 1-4`, not `DR 01-04` or `CCANDR01-04`).

## Commission orders

**First citation:** case number, italic case caption, order number, pinpoint, Maillog number, date, then any parenthetical.

> Case No. 9705, _The 2024-2026 EmPOWER Maryland Program_, Order No. 91461, at 9, Maillog No. 314502 (Dec. 27, 2024) (approving revised 2025–2026 program plans).

**Later citations:** `Order No. 91461 at 15.`

Write the Maillog number as `Maillog No. 314502` _(standardized: the filed testimony once wrote `ML 333852`)_.

## Utility filings in other dockets

> Case No. 9890, _The 2027-2029 EmPOWER Maryland Program_, BGE's Proposed EmPOWER Maryland Program for 2027 – 2029, at 13, Maillog No. 333852 (Sep. 1, 2026).

These follow the order pattern: case number, italic caption, the filing's own title, pinpoint, Maillog number, date.

## Tariffs

> BGE tariff, P.S.C. Md. — E-6, Rider 1, Sheet 77-A (Supplement 751, filed May 18, 2026, effective June 1, 2026).

Give the schedule or rider, the sheet, and in the parenthetical the supplement number written out, with its filed and effective dates. If the tariff is also one of your exhibits, add it after the parenthetical: `…, effective June 1, 2026), Ex. JPV-10.`

## Statutes, bills and executive orders

- **Statutes:** `Md. Code Ann., Pub. Util. § 2-113(a)(2)(v)–(vi).` Several sections: `Md. Code Ann., Envir. §§ 2-1204.1 and 2-1204.2.` A named act gives its name and bill first: `Climate Solutions Now Act of 2022 (Senate Bill 528), Md. Code Ann., Envir. §§ 2-1204.1 and 2-1204.2.`
- **Bills:** `House Bill 864, 2024 Regular Session, Maryland General Assembly (2024).`
- **Executive orders:** `Maryland Executive Order 01.01.2024.19, Leadership by State Government: Implementing Maryland's Climate Pollution Reduction Plan, at Section C.2.b (June 4, 2024).`

## Reports, studies, articles and web pages

Write the source out in full in the footnote: author or organization, italic title, pinpoint, date, URL.

- **Reports:** `Maryland Department of the Environment, _Maryland's Climate Pollution Reduction Plan_, at 39 (Dec. 2023), https://….` With a named author: `Shah, Wagner & Teener, _Designing Better Rates for Heat Pump Customers_, RMI (June 17, 2026).`
- **Books:** `James C. Bonbright, _Principles of Public Utility Rates_, New York, NY: Columbia University Press, at 291 (1961), https://….`
- **Journal articles:** `Wanyu R. Chan et al., Analysis of Air Leakage Measurements of US Houses, 66 _Energy and Buildings_ 616–25 (Nov. 2013), https://doi.org/….` The volume goes before the italic journal name and the first page after it _(standardized: the filed testimony put a comma after the journal name)_.
- **News and web articles:** `Mona Zhang, "How NY heat pump users got stuck with higher bills," _E&E News_ (Mar. 3, 2026).` The article title goes in quotes and the outlet in italics.
- **Web pages and databases:** `Arcadia, _Tariff Database_, https://… (last accessed Sep. 21, 2026).`
- **Datasets:** describe what was used, then the source: `Computed from NREL's ResStock 2024 building stock model for Maryland, restricted to buildings assigned to BGE's service territory. NREL, _End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock_ (2024), https://doi.org/10.25984/1876417.`

## Signals, repeats and cross-references

- **Signals are italic:** `_See_`, `_See also_`, `_Cf._`. Capitalize the signal at the start of a footnote and lowercase it after a semicolon: `_See_ Ex. JPV-2, …; _see also_ Karas Direct at 21:11–22:7.`
- **`_Id._`** replaces the full citation when the footnote immediately before cites only that same source: `_Id._` (same page) or `_Id._ at 41.`
- **`_supra_` and `_infra_`** point to other footnotes and sections: `_See supra_ n.8.`, `_See infra_ Section X.B.1.`, `_see also infra_ Section X.B.2.`
- **Several sources in one footnote** are separated by semicolons.

## Parentheticals

An explanation or a quotation goes in parentheses after the citation it describes:

> Karas Direct at 4:4–8 ("An effective rate design incorporates the principles of cost causation, …").

> Ex. JPV-6, BGE Response to CCAN DR 1-8 (BGE "does not track customers by known heat source").

Sentences that comment on the source come after the citation, as ordinary prose.

## Dates

Abbreviate months as `Jan.`, `Feb.`, `Mar.`, `Apr.`, `Aug.`, `Sep.`, `Oct.`, `Nov.`, `Dec.`; write out `May`, `June` and `July`. Examples: `(Dec. 27, 2024)`, `(Sep. 1, 2026)`, `(July 2, 2026)`.

## Links

Link every citation of a library source to its public copy through the source's short link, `https://pub.switch.box/<citekey>` (the extract's `short_url` front matter, or the source's `url` in `reports/references.yaml`). Link the document's name, and keep the pinpoint, date and parenthetical outside the link:

> [Rebuttal Testimony of Jennifer A. Karas on behalf of Baltimore Gas and Electric Company](https://pub.switch.box/karas_Docket9888Prepared_2026) ("Karas Rebuttal"), at 22:1–2 (Oct. 16, 2026).

> [Karas Rebuttal](https://pub.switch.box/karas_Docket9888Prepared_2026) at 22:6–8.

> [BGE Response to CCAN DR 1-8](https://pub.switch.box/bge_Docket9888Response_2026) (BGE "does not track customers by known heat source").

- Short links redirect to the whole document, so they cannot carry the page; the pinpoint does that.
- Link later citations and `_Id._` too, so a reader can open the source from any footnote.
- A source with no short link has no public copy yet. Ingest it first (the `ingest-source` skill) rather than linking to a filing portal or a private file; if that has to wait, mark the footnote for the user.
- A source's own URL (a statute on the legislature's site, a web page) is fine when the source is not in the library.

## Citation keys

Put the source's citation key in each footnote that cites a library source, once, right after the citation it supports: `… at 4:13–14 (July 2, 2026) [@karas_Docket9888Direct_2026].` Give the key no locator; the pinpoint is already in the text.

The keys exist for the bibliography tooling and the HTML preview. The Word export (`just testimony`, via `lib/just/templates/filters/testimony.lua`) removes them, so the filed document never shows author-date tags like "(Karas 2026)". Write every citation so it reads completely without its key.

Testimony has no References section: the footnotes are the citations. Do not add `## References` or `:::{#refs}`; the Word export drops any reference list.
