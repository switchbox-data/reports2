# Docket 25-55: Heat Pump Rate Design (Exhibit ES-HP-2)

Exhibit ES-HP-2, Eversource's rate design workbook, filed 4/17/25 with Richard Chin's direct testimony (ES-HP-1) in D.P.U. 25-55 to support the R-1HP/R-2HP rates the DPU's D.P.U. 25-08 order told the company to file. It derives revenue-neutral seasonal charges, converts gas heating therms into heat pump kWh, and shows bill impacts, all as live formulas. We keep it to see how a utility modeled a typical heat pump customer from gas load.

## Sheets

- **Sch 1_Heat Pump Rate Design** (43 rows x 4 columns): 1
- **Sch 2_HP Sales Conversion** (27 rows x 19 columns): 1
- **Sch 3_Bill Impact_ASHP** (26 rows x 17 columns): 1
- **WP_Electric Test Year BQs** (100 rows x 19 columns): NSTAR Electric Company
- **WP_Gas Test Year BQs** (50 rows x 17 columns): NSTAR Gas Company
- **WP_Sales Forecast** (123 rows x 17 columns): 2025 Sales kWh Forecast
- **WP_Efficiency Factors** (22 rows x 3 columns): Efficiency Factors
- **WP_Rate Inputs** (35 rows x 6 columns): Rate Inputs
