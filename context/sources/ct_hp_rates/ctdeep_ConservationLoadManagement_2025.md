# Conservation and Load Management (C&LM)

##### **What is the Conservation and Load Management Plan?**

The Conservation and Load Management Plan (C&LM Plan) is an energy efficiency and demand management investment plan that develops programs and initiatives to help Connecticut residents and businesses become more energy efficient.

These programs and services are administered and delivered by the Utilities through Energize CT. To learn about energy-saving opportunities for your home or business, visit the website [EnergizeCT.com](https://www.energizect.com/) .

##### **How does State Energy Efficiency Planning work?**

The Plan is updated annually by Connecticut’s electric and gas utilities, with advisory and assistance from the[CT Energy Efficiency Board](https://www.energizect.com/connecticut-energy-efficiency-board)  (EEB). DEEP reviews all C&LM Plans and associated budgets in order to achieve cost-effective energy savings across the state.
The Plan is funded by various sources, including a charge on customer energy bills, the [Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative](https://portal.ct.gov/DEEP/Air/Climate-Change/Regional-Greenhouse-Gas-Initiative-RGGI) (RGGI) and the proceeds from C&LM savings sold into the [ISO New England Forward Capacity Market](https://www.iso-ne.com/markets-operations/markets/forward-capacity-market/) .

### Current and Approved Plans

The Conservation & Load Management (C&LM)Plans are developed every three years and updated annually. Three-year Plans and annual Plan Updates are developed by the utilities, with guidance from the Energy Efficiency Board, public input, and approval from DEEP. The [2025-2027 Plan](https://www.dpuc.state.ct.us/DEEPEnergy.nsf/c6c6d525f7cdd1168525797d0047c5bf/6b31d3583406937085258bce00697bcf?OpenDocument) was filed by the utilities on Nov. 1, 2024. On December 20, 2024, DEEP approved the proposed budget for the 2025-2027 C&LM Plan.  On September 9, 2025, DEEP approved the [Final Determination of the 2025-2027 Plan](https://www.dpuc.state.ct.us/DEEPEnergy.nsf/c6c6d525f7cdd1168525797d0047c5bf/ce2d1746ee5e349b85258d00006877c2?OpenDocument) .

#### Related Initiatives

**Equitable Energy Efficiency (E3) Proceeding**

DEEP launched a formal proceeding to make its energy efficiency programs more equitable and inclusive. [**Learn more about E3**](https://portal.ct.gov/deep/energy/conservation-and-load-management/equitable-energy-efficiency).

###### **Weatherization Barrier Remediation**

Addressing health and safety issues, such as mold and asbestos, that prevent the completion of residential weatherization and energy efficiency measures. [Learn more about Weatherization Barrier Remediation](https://portal.ct.gov/DEEP/Energy/Conservation-and-Load-Management/Weatherization-Barrier-Mitigation "https://portal.ct.gov/DEEP/Energy/Conservation-and-Load-Management/Weatherization-Barrier-Mitigation")

###### **Contractor Technical Advisory Committee (CTAC)**

DEEP hosts regular meetings to interface with energy efficiency contractors. Members of the public and other interested parties are welcome to attend and provide feedback to guide future decision-making. [Learn more about CTAC](https://portal.ct.gov/deep/energy/conservation-and-load-management/ctac)

###### **Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP)**

The US Department of Energy funds the Weatherization Assistance Program and helps low-income people reduce energy costs and fuel use in their homes through retrofits and home improvements. [Learn more about WAP](https://portal.ct.gov/deep/energy/weatherization/weatherization-in-connecticut)
