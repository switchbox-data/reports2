Versant Power ("Versant" or "the Company"); the Office of the Public Advocate (the "OPA"); the Governor's Energy Office (the "GEO"); ChargePoint, Inc. ("ChargePoint"); the Conservation Law Foundation (the "CLF"); the Natural Resources Council of Maine (the "NRCM"); the Acadia Center ("Acadia"); Efficiency Maine Trust ("EMT") hereby agree and stipulate as follows:

## I. PURPOSE

It is the intent of this Stipulation to settle all issues in this proceeding and to expedite the Maine Public Utilities Commission's (the "Commission") consideration and resolution of rate designs consistent with the State of Maine's policies to combat climate change and to support beneficial electrification of transportation, space heating, and energy storage. The rates instituted in this Stipulation are proposed for implementation pending availability of additional data from Versant's new metering systems being installed currently and design of advanced distribution management or similar system in other Commission dockets over the next three years. The purpose of this Stipulation is to present a settlement to put in place advanced time varying rates for Versant. The rates updated in this stipulation will support customer use of electric vehicles, electric heat pump technologies, and electric storage technologies. The provisions agreed to herein have been reached as a result of information gathered through discovery and discussions among the parties in this case.

## II. PROCEDURAL BACKGROUND

On October 22, 2021, the Commission filed the Notice of Investigation of this docket. By November 12, 2021, the GEO, Office of Innovation & Policy and the OPA had filed to intervene. On December 1, 2021 Central Maine Power ("CMP"), Fox Islands Electric Cooperative ("Fox Islands"), and Versant Power filed comments and proposed tariffs to combat climate change and to support beneficial electrification of transportation, space heating, and energy storage. On December 2, 2021 CES filed for intervention and on December 3, 2021 EMEC filed its proposed tariffs. By December 20, 2021 petitions to intervene were filed by the NRC, CLF, Acadia, EMT, NECEC, MREA and the Transit Agencies. The Transit Agencies submitted comments on December 22, 2021.

The Initial Case conference was held January 4, 2022 and a Scheduling Order subsequently issued that day. Pursuant to the Scheduling Order, data requests were submitted to CMP, Fox Islands and Versant. On January 11, 2022, Madison Electric Works ("MEW") submitted a late-filed response to the Commission's Notice of Investigation, followed by IECG's late-filed petition to intervene on January 18, 2022. CMP, Fox Islands and Versant responded to the data requests by January 25, 2022. Additionally on January 25, 2022 Versant submitted a revised proposed tariff and on January 27, 2022 CMP submitted its updated proposed tariff. On February 1, 2022 Casco Bay Island Transit District ("CBITD") submitted a late-filed petition to intervene, followed by the late-filed petition to intervene filed by ChargePoint on February 4, 2022.

On February 28, 2022, the Commission issued a procedural order rescheduling a technical conference and requesting additional information from CMP to which CMP responded on March 3, 2022. A Technical Conference was held March 4, 2022. Versant updated the record by filing

<!-- PDF page 3 (printed page 3) -->

the 2015 Cost of Service Study. On March 18, 2022, the Commission released the Straw Proposal. By April 25, 2022, the Maine Transit Association (the "MTA"), the Transit Agencies, the OPA, CES, the CLF, ChargePoint, CMP, Acadia, Versant, EMT, and the GEO submitted comments on the Straw Proposal.

On April 26, 2022, based on a meaningful amount of agreement, the Commission converted the scheduled workshop into a settlement conference. On April 27, 2022, the Transit Agencies and CES submitted concerns and objections to the April 26, 2022 procedural order. On April 29, 2022, the settlement conference was held.

On May 2, 2022, a procedural order was filed setting the next date for settlement discussions. On May 4, 2022, the Commission approved CMP's proposed tariffs filed in this docket. The Commission held a second and final settlement conference on May 13, 2022. Following that conference, the Commission asked the parties to work together to commit the areas of agreement into propose separate settlement agreements for CMP and Versant each in the form of a proposed stipulation to the Commission for consideration.

### L. Continued Stakeholder Discussion on Advanced Rate Designs

1. <u>Ongoing Discussion on Advanced Rate Design.</u>

   (a) The parties agree to continue discussions on ongoing rate design improvements to rates for new advanced technologies for transportation, space heating and cooling, and energy storage;

   (b) As Versant has data available from an operational AMI system and other data source, Versant will share hourly system peak data and discuss with stakeholders different methods for identifying the hours of system peak load and end uses contributing to such peak load consistent with customer privacy;

   (c) As Versant has data available from an operational AMI system, Versant may be able to examine the diversity of circuit peaks across its system to assess the feasibility of more advanced rates system-wide;

   (d) With the aim of developing advanced rate designs collaboratively, Versant agrees to provide recommended updates to its time of use periods and whether to modify its seasonally differentiated heating distribution rates from the rates adopted in this settlement.

2. <u>Party Agreement to Consider More Advanced Rate Designs.</u> Based on analysis of system peaking hours data and other load data, the Parties to this Stipulation agree to consider more advanced rate designs for proposal to the Commission consistent with cost-causation and Maine's public policy goals for decarbonization.
