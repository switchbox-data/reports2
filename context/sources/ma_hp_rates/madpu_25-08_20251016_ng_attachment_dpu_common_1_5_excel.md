# Docket 25-08: Attachment DPU-Common 1-5-NG, Calculation of Illustrative Transmission Heat Pump Rates

Attachment to National Grid's October 16, 2025 answer to DPU-Common 1-5 in D.P.U. 25-08, which asked for heat pump rate calculations with hypothetical winter transmission pricing in the format of Eversource's Exhibit ES-HP-2; prepared under Polina Demers. Three sheets of live formulas give an illustrative $0.02120 winter charge and the 2.74 factor from Exhibit NG-4 page 1. The first transmission version of National Grid's design, a template for D.P.U. 26-71.

## Sheets

- **Transmission Rate Design** (46 rows x 5 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Adjusted Forecast** (40 rows x 10 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
- **Heat Pump Rate Design** (75 rows x 19 columns): Massachusetts Electric Company
