# Maryland Rebates

## BGE (Baltimore Gas & Electric)

**Eligible Areas:** Residential customers in [BGE service areas](https://www.bge.com/about-us/company-information)

**Program Dates:** Ongoing

**Rebate Type:** Midstream (Incentive is issued to the Distributor, with payment or credit to the contractor.)

**Qualifying Equipment:**

_†HVAC Distributors may retain up to $50 of the rebate amount as a processing fee._

**Reimbursement:**The contractor must apply online at: <https://bgemidstream.sightline-icf.com>
_Note: If you do not have a login, please contact marylandmidstream@icf.com with to obtain access._

**Application Deadline:** Within 30 days of equipment install date

**Documentation Required:**

- Completion of online application including:
  - BGE customer installation address and associated account #
  - Detail regarding purchased and installed equipment including unit type, model, serial number, and program incentive amounts applied to the customer sale
- Contractor Invoice w/ Instant Rebate noted as individual line item **BGE Residential HVAC Rebate**
- AHRI Certificate # (Optional)

## Potomac Edison

**Eligible Areas:** Residential electric customers of Potomac Edison MD

**Program Dates:** Ongoing

**Rebate Type:** Midstream (Incentive is issued to the Distributor, with payment or credit to the contractor.)

**Qualifying Equipment:**

[Click here](https://wp.hvacdist.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/HVAC-Measure-List-22.pdf) for qualifying equipment as of August 1, 2025._\*_

_\*HVAC Distributors may retain up to $100 of the rebate amount as a processing fee._

_**NEW**_ – Enhance your customer proposals by [including an insert](https://energysavemd-home.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FEMD_HVAC_Contractor_DigitalFlyer_FEB2025_2.pdf?utm_campaign=16538492-FE%20MD%202025%20Contractor%20Newsletters&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8d14r3VU-7A-GN-MyR3oUT5WXSUaizpQOffulnMQqCC-d-hfV3_fLfQDaNF3W2jKD5FVt90PdgJoa54aJAvUa59IN8Wg&_hsmi=370888744&utm_content=370888744&utm_source=hs_email) to showcase your credentials as a participating contractor in our Residential HVAC Program and highlight the benefits of investing in high-efficiency HVAC equipment.

**Reimbursement:** The contractor must apply online at: <https://energysavemd-home.com/participating-contractors/contractor-resources/>
_Note: If the contractor does not have a login, they can request access [here](https://energysavemd-home.com/participating-contractors/)._

**Application Deadline:** Submissions are required within 90 days of the installation date

**Documentation Required:**

- A distributor sales receipt indicating the type of equipment, purchase price, model and serial numbers (bulk purchase receipts will be accepted)
- An AHRI certificate or manufacturer’s specification sheet including model number, capacity and efficiency performance rating
- Customer Invoice, including:
  - Model Number
  - Serial Number
  - Installation price for each measure
  - The invoice total must have all or a portion of the Potomac Edison incentive deducted from the final cost.
  - The program attribution line should read as follows: _Discount brought to you by the Potomac Edison – MD Residential Energy Efficiency Program in support of EmPOWER Maryland_
- If a new furnace was installed with CAC or HP the model detail must be included on the AHRI. This information is required to qualify.
- Please include all of the required model number and serial numbers (indoor coil & outdoor condenser) to avoid rejections.
