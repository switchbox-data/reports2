# Form EIA-861 Detailed Data Files: Advanced Meters, 2025 Data Year

EIA's annual census of U.S. electric utilities (Form EIA-861), final 2025 data released Oct. 7, 2026; this is the Advanced Meters file. Lists each utility's AMR, AMI and standard meters by sector, plus AMI energy served. Read for BGE's share of residential meters that are AMI (98.1%, year-end 2025), cited in CCAN's Docket 9888 surrebuttal.

## Sheets

- **states** (2741 rows x 47 columns): Utility Characteristics, Number AMR- Automated Meter Reading, Number AMI- Advanced Metering Infrastructure, Number Home Area Network, Number Standard (non AMR/AMI) Meters, Total Numbers of Meters, Energy Served - AMI (MWh), Customers with Daily Digital Access, Customers with Direct Load Control
- **territories** (10 rows x 47 columns): Utility Characteristics, Number AMR- Automated Meter Reading, Number AMI- Advanced Metering Infrastructure, Number Home Area Network, Number Standard (non AMR/AMI) Meters, Total Numbers of Meters, Energy Served - AMI (MWh), Customers with Daily Digital Access, Customers with Direct Load Control
