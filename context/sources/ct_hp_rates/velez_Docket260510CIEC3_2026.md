# Docket 26-05-10: CIEC-3(c) Workpaper, Rate 1 Charge Increase

Workpaper supporting CLF-SC's response to CIEC-3(c) in the Eversource Connecticut rate case. Divides the net revenue reduction from Rate HP enrollment by the remaining Rate 1 customers' weighted kWh to compute the per-kWh increase and its share of the Rate 1 volumetric rate. Read for the formula chain behind the Rate 1 increase tables.
