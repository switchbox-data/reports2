### 2nd Revised Sheet No. 483 (Canceling 1st Revised Sheet No. 483)

**RATE BEST — BASIC ELECTRIC SERVICE TIME OF USE PRICING**

**[\*]** **APPLICABILITY.**

Residential retail customers may begin taking service under this tariff beginning with the June 2026 monthly billing period and eligible nonresidential retail customers may begin taking service under this tariff beginning with the January 2028 monthly billing period. Service hereunder is not applicable to a retail customer to which the Competitively Declared Customer Group is applicable.

This tariff is available to residential retail customers for which (a) the Company will be providing bundled electric service that includes distribution rates provided under time of use differentiated Distribution Facilities Charges, (b) service is not taken under Service Classifications NM1, NM2, NM3, or NM4 of Rider POGNM – Parallel Operation of Retail Customer Generating Facilities with Net Metering (Rider POGNM), or Rider NAM – Non AMI Metering (Rider NAM), (c) successful enrollment for service under this tariff has occurred using the enrollment process established by the Company, and (d) advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) meter capable of providing interval data is installed for all metered points of service.

This tariff is available to nonresidential retail customers for which (a) the Company will be providing bundled electric service that includes applicable Distribution Facilities Charges, (b) service is not taken under Service Classifications NM1, NM2, NM3, NM4, NM5, NM6, NM11 or NM12 of Rider POGNM, or Rider NAM, (c) successful enrollment for service under this tariff has occurred using the enrollment process established by the Company, and (d) service points with advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) meters capable of providing interval data.

**PURPOSE.**

The purpose of this tariff is to provide eligible retail customers with a bundled electric service option that (a) encourages less electricity usage, or a shift in electricity usage, from periods when electricity prices are typically higher in the hourly wholesale electricity market, (b) encourages less electricity use during the periods when the distribution and transmission systems incur their peak demands, (c) encourages electric vehicle owners to charge their electric vehicles in the early morning hours when hourly wholesale electricity prices are typically lower, and (d) increases customers' satisfaction with their electric service with a new supply rate option.

**DEFINITIONS.**

Generally, definitions of terms used in this tariff are provided in the Definitions part of the General Terms and Conditions of the Company's Schedule of Rates. The following acronyms and terms are defined for use in this tariff:

**Basic Electric Service Time of Use Pricing (BEST)**
Basic Electric Service Time of Use Pricing means the bundled electric service rate provided under the provisions of this tariff.

**[\*]** **BEST Customer Classes**
Customer Classes are designations for retail customers served under Rate BEST for computing charges for electric power and energy and applying such charges to retail customers. The following customer classes are defined for such purposes:

**Residential BEST Customer Class**
Residential BEST Customer Class is any residential retail customer that meets the eligibility requirements in the Applicability section of Rate BEST and is in the Residential Customer Group.

**Nonresidential BEST Customer Class**
Nonresidential BEST Customer Class is any nonresidential retail customer that meets the eligibility requirements of the Applicability section of Rate BEST and is in either the Watt-Hour Customer Group or the Demand Customer Group.

**BEST Purchased Electricity Adjustment Factor**
BEST Purchased Electricity Adjustment Factor (TPEA) means a unit credit or charge that, through its application, periodically equalizes applicable revenues from retail customers taking service hereunder for electric power and energy procured for them by the Company and the expenses incurred by the Company related to the procurement of such electric power and energy, thereby ensuring that the Company does not over or under recover its costs related to the procurement of electric power and energy for its retail customers.

**Capacity Costs**
Capacity costs means capacity obligation costs incurred by the Company to procure electric power and energy directly from PJM-administered markets for customers receiving service hereunder.

**[\*]** **Electricity Charges**

Each year after the initial implementation of the Electricity Charges, the Electricity Charges are updated and will be applicable beginning with that year's June monthly billing period and extending through the following May monthly billing period. Notwithstanding such periodic update, the Electricity Charges may be revised by the Company if the Company determines such revision results in a better match between the Company's applicable expected costs and its recovery of those costs.

Electricity Charges are applicable to each kWh provided to the retail customer during the corresponding Morning Period, Mid-Day Peak Period, Evening Period or Overnight Period in the summer or nonsummer monthly billing period for which such charge is determined. Each such summer and nonsummer MPEC, MDPPEC, EPEC and OPEC is determined in the following two steps and formulas: (1) the first step, Formula 1, determines the per kWh energy charge, for each Rate BEST Customer Class (C), that would have been recovered with the historical PJM real time residual locational marginal prices for the ComEd Zone and distribution losses for the participants' aggregate illustrative profile for each of the summer and nonsummer price periods, and (2) the second step, Formula 2, takes the corresponding per kWh energy charges for each Rate BEST Customer Class, C, and adds any associated per kWh adjustments to make the charges reflective of current or future market prices for each period, converts the charges to cents/kWh and applies the uncollectible factors to determine the resultant charge:

**Formula 1:**

$$
HENC_{C,P,S/N} = \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{\text{Start Hour}}^{\text{End Hour}} \left[HkW_C \times HLMP\right] \times \frac{1\ \text{MWh}}{1{,}000\ \text{kWh}} \times \left(1 + DLF_C\right)}{E_{C,P,S/N}}
$$

**Formula 2:**

$$
EC_{C,P,S/N} = \left(HENC_{C,P,S/N} + A_{C,P,S/N}\right) \times 100¢/\$ \times SBUF \times ISUF
$$

**BEST Purchased Electricity Adjustment Factor**

The BEST Purchased Electricity Adjustment Factor (TPEA) periodically equalizes the revenues from retail customers receiving electric service under this tariff for electric power and energy procured for them by the Company and the expenses incurred by the Company related to the procurement of such electric power and energy as provided in this Monthly Charges section.

A TPEA, in ¢/kWh rounded to the thousandths of a cent, is determined and applied to each kWh provided to the retail customer during a given effective period. The TPEA is shown as a separate line item on the retail customer's monthly bill for electric service and designated as the Purchased Electricity Adjustment. The TPEA is determined and applied in such effective period in accordance with the following equation:

$$
TPEA = \frac{\left[AE - AR + AB + A\right]_{\text{amortized}} + \sum ATPEA}{U} \times \frac{100¢}{\$1}
$$

Where:

- $TPEA$ = BEST Purchased Electricity Adjustment Factor, in ¢/kWh rounded to the thousandths of a cent, applied as a credit or charge to kWhs provided to retail customers receiving electric service under this tariff during the effective period.
