**Request No. DPU 2-04:**

Refer to the Residential Heat Pump Service Illustrative Tariff, Schedule HP-RES and the Low-Income Residential Heat Pump Service Illustrative Tariff, Schedule HP-RES LI. Please explain how the Company proposes to enforce the availability clauses of its proposed heat pump services tariff.

**Response:**

As described in the Tariff Schedules noted above, service under these rates will be contingent upon a "customer affirming ownership of a heat pump installed at the service location." In addition, the heat pump installed must be capable of and used to "heat or supplement another heat source for their home or part of their home."

The Company will accept a customer's attestation that they have a Cold Climate Heat Pump ("CCHP") installed in their home, sized appropriately to either fully heat the home or supplement another heat source in the home and that they intend to use the heat pump as their primary or supplemental source of heat.

The Company will review each customer on this rate annually to determine if the customer's usage is consistent with the use of a CCHP as the primary or supplemental source of heat.
