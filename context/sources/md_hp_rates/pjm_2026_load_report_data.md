# 2026 Load Report Data: Monthly Peak and Energy Forecasts by Zone

PJM's data workbook released with its January 2026 Load Forecast Report. One sheet per transmission zone and region (BGE, PEPCO, PJM RTO and others) gives forecast monthly peak load and energy for 2026 to 2046. Read for BGE zone summer versus winter peaks, which stay summer peaking through 2046.
