Dear Mr. Lanphear:

On September 27, 2022, the Commission issued its Order Approving Stipulation (CMP) ('the Order") in the above-referenced proceeding. As part of the Order, the Commission approved a Revised Stipulation dated September 22, 2022. In approving the Revised Stipulation, the Commission approved proposed revisions to several of Central Maine Power Company's ("CMP" or the "Company") Electric Delivery Rate Schedules, namely Rate A-LM, Rate A-TOU-OPTS, and Rate B-DCFC. As stated in the Revised Stipulation, the Stipulating Parties agreed that the revised Rate A-TOU-OPTS is an optional rate intended only as an interim whole house rate to encourage adoption of beneficial electrification technologies and that a long-term rate for this purpose should be designed to more accurately reflect the potential contribution to peak demand of consumption by customers on a rate offered for this purpose. In addition, Paragraph 9 of the Revised Stipulation provides:

> Based on the results of discussions among the Stipulating Parties, the Stipulating Parties agree that in addition to the rate design changes prescribed above, CMP will continue to collaborate with the Stipulating Parties to develop an additional new pilot residential heat pump rate. Any proposed tariff for this rate will be filed by CMP no later than October 7, 2022. The proposed pilot residential heat pump rate may be approved by the Director of Technical Analysis. The proceeding shall remain open to allow resolution of the proposed pilot additional residential heat pump rate. The pilot rate will sunset automatically with the adoption of a replacement residential beneficial electrification rate or rates by the Commission.

In accordance with Paragraph 9 of the Revised Stipulation, CMP has worked with other Stipulating Parties to develop the proposed attached Rate A – Seasonal, which would be available to customers verbally affirming heat pump ownership at their service location. This proposed rate would have a monthly Service Fee of $31.67

<!-- PDF page 2 -->

. The kWh charge from May through October would be $0.161867 per kWh, and the kWh charge for the remainder of the year would be $0.003478 per kWh. This rate was designed to be revenue neutral when compared to the revised Rate A-TOU-OPTS rate approved in this proceeding. This would be a pilot rate, which would be available to no more than 5,000 customers. The rate would automatically terminate on or upon the earlier of October 31, 2024 or the adoption of a replacement residential beneficial electrification rate or rates by the Commission. Although availability of this rate is predicated on a customer electing to take service under this rate on a year-round basis for a period of at least twelve months, a customer would be allowed to opt out of this rate before the required twelve-month period is complete, provided that any such customer would not be eligible to participate in this rate for a period of one year from the date on which service under this rate was terminated.

This proposed rate has been circulated to the parties to this proceeding via electronic mail. CMP has received limited feedback, which feedback has been incorporated into the proposed Rate A – Residential Electric Delivery Rate Schedule attached hereto. Furthermore, CMP has received no feedback adverse to the proposed rate.

CMP submits that the proposed pilot rate is just and reasonable in that it is revenue neutral when compared to the revised Rate A-TOU-OPTS, which is the other Commission-approved rate designed to encourage heat pump use, and it may provide a benefit to all customers by encouraging additional sales and thereby reducing rates.

CMP requests that the Commission approve this new rate schedule with an effective date of January 1, 2023. However, in order for CMP to be able to program this new rate in time for a January 1, 2023 effective date, CMP requests approval by November 1, 2022.

Please contact me if the Commission has any questions regarding this filing.
