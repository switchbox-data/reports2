## CCANDR04-03

**Item No.: CCANDR04-03**

What percentage of substations within BGE’s service territory currently have more than 20% headroom (the difference between their rated capacity and their annual peak, whether it occurs in winter or summer)?

**RESPONSE:**

62% of substations currently have more than 20% of headroom. Headroom, in this response, is defined as the percentage of available capacity for a substation.
