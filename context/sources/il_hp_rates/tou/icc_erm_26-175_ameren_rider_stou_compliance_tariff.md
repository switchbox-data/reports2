**APPLICABILITY**

**[\*]** Rider PER – Purchased Electricity Recovery (Rider PER) is applicable to Customers taking service under Rider BGS – Basic Generation Service (Rate BGS). This Rider is also applicable to Customers taking service under Rider RTP – Real-Time Pricing (Rider RTP) and Rider STOU – Supply Time-of-Use (Rider STOU).

**PURPOSE**

**[\*]** Bundled electric service is the provision to the Customer of electric power and energy by the Company. Such provision includes the procurement of all the component services the Company requires to meet the instantaneous electric power and energy requirements at any given time of applicable Customers under Company's tariffs, applicable tariffs on file with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC), and other applicable law, including, without limitation, all required electric energy, energy to satisfy losses, electric generation capacity, volumetric risk management, transmission services, ancillary transmission services, administrative services, and any other services necessary, including collateral payments or other forms of security paid or obligated to suppliers or MISO, or its successor in interest, for the provision of electric power and energy. In accordance with Section 16-111.5(l) of the Public Utilities Act (Act), the primary purpose of this Rider is to allow the Company to recover all the costs it incurs to procure all such component services not recovered through other mechanisms.

Other purposes of this Rider are to set forth the Company's obligations with respect to (a) the planning process associated with procurement plans in accordance with Section 16-111.5(d)(1) of the Act; (b) the contingency procurement of electric power and energy in accordance with Sections 16-111.5(e)(5)(i) and 16-111.5(e)(5)(iii) of the Act; and (c) the mechanisms employed to ensure that the Company does not over or under recover the costs it incurs in procuring electric power and energy for Customers taking service under tariffs to which this Rider is applicable.

**DEFINITIONS**

Definitions of terms used in this Rider are provided in the Customer Terms and Conditions.

### 3rd Revised Sheet No. 25.012 (Canceling 2nd Revised Sheet No. 25.012)

**PURCHASED ELECTRICITY ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM**

**[\*]** In accordance with Section 16-111.5 of the Act that states the application of the retail purchased electricity charges must not result in over or under recovery of the Company's costs to procure electric power and energy for its Customers "due to changes in Customer usage and demand patterns," the Purchased Electricity Adjustment mechanism periodically equalizes the revenues from Customers taking service under Rider BGS, Rider RTP, and Rider STOU as appropriate, for electric power and energy supply procured for them by the Company and the expenses incurred by the Company to procure such electric power and energy supply. The PEA, in ¢/kWh rounded to the thousandths of a cent, is determined and applied to each kWh provided by the Company under Rider BGS, Rider RTP, and Rider STOU as appropriate, on a monthly basis. The PEA will be calculated by Rate Zone through the May 2012 Determination Period. Thereafter, the Accrued Expense (AE) and Accrued Revenue (AR) components of the PEA will no longer be separated by Rate Zones. Differences in the PEA applied to each Rate Zone for the amounts related to the Automatic Balancing (AB) and Adjustment (A) amounts on or before the May 2012 Determination Period may occur through the amortization period. The Company will amortize the AB amounts as of May 2012 over a period not to exceed 12 months starting in the June 2012 Determination Period, and will calculate a rate that will be added to the non-Rate Zone differentiated PEA factor. Subsequent A amounts that relate to a Determination Period on or before May 2012 will be amortized over a period not to exceed 12 Effective Periods. The rate derived from the A amount will be added to the non-Rate Zone differentiated PEA factor.

The PEA factor is determined and applied in each Effective Period in accordance with the following equation:

**PEA equation:**

$$
PEA = \frac{\left\{\left[AE - AR + AB + A\right]_{\text{amortized}} + SA\right\}}{U} \times \frac{100¢}{\$1}
$$

Where:

**[\*]** $PEA$ = Purchased Electricity Adjustment, in ¢/kWh rounded to the thousandths of a cent, applied as a credit or charge to kWhs provided to Customers taking service under Rider BGS, Rider RTP, and Rider STOU as appropriate, during the Effective Period.

**[\*]** $AE$ = Accrued Expenses, in dollars, equal to the sum of the accrued expenses incurred by the Company in accordance with this Rider for the electric power and energy procured for Customers taking service under Rider BGS, Rider RTP, and Rider STOU as appropriate, during the Determination Period(s). **[\*]** **RIDER STOU – SUPPLY TIME-OF-USE**

**[\*]** **AVAILABILITY**

This Rider is available to any Customer served under DS-1 or DS-2 where the Customer elects to purchase Company-supplied power and energy under supply time-of-use, the Customer has given the Company the required notification as specified in the Customer Terms and Conditions, and has the necessary metering installed.

A DS-1 (Residential) or DS-2 (General Service) Customer that has terminated service under this Rider will not be eligible to reinstate service under this Rider for a period of 12 months after the date of termination.

**PURPOSE**

The purpose of this tariff is to comply with the requirements in 220 ILCS 5/16-107.8, specifically to establish an optional market-based time-of-use supply rate – Rider STOU – Supply Time-Of-Use (STOU). Rider STOU, in combination with Delivery Service (DS) and Transmission Service (TS) tariffs contained within this Schedule, represent a Bundled Service option available to Customers served on Rate DS-1 – Residential Delivery Service (DS-1) and Rate DS-2 – Small General Delivery Service (DS-2) that elect to purchase electric power and energy supply from the Company. The electric power and energy charges determined under this Rider shall include all applicable MISO energy, capacity, fees and other charges.

**DEFINITIONS**

**MISO Planning Year**
MISO Planning Year means the 12 consecutive month period beginning June 1 of the subject year.

**Non-Summer**
Non-Summer means the months October through May.

**STOU Periods**
STOU Periods means the periods of time in which energy consumption occurs. For the purposes of this tariff, three distinct periods shall exist: off-peak, intermediate, and on-peak. The STOU Periods shall be provided in the most currently effective informational filing made pursuant to this Rider.

**Summer**
Summer means the months June through September.

> The tariff itself does not state the hours of the three STOU periods; they are set in the Rider STOU informational filing (not part of this PDF).

### 3rd Revised Sheet No. 29.002 (Canceling 2nd Revised Sheet No. 29.002)

**[\*]** **RIDER STOU – SUPPLY TIME-OF-USE**

**[\*]** **Step 1:**

$$
ESTOUR_{P,V,S\text{ or }N} = EP_{P,S\text{ or }N} \times DLA_V
$$

**Step 2:**

$$
CSTOUR_{P,S\text{ or }N} = \frac{CC_{S\text{ or }N} \times PLC_{S\text{ or }N} \times \text{Seasonal Days}_{S\text{ or }N}}{PkWh_{S\text{ or }N}}
$$

_(The "=" sign is missing in the source's Step 2 formula; it has been supplied here.)_

**Step 3:**

$$
STOUR_{P,V,S\text{ or }N} = ESTOUR_{P,V,S\text{ or }N} + CSTOUR_{P,S\text{ or }N} + TSC_{STOU}
$$

Where:

- $STOUR_{P,V,S\text{ or }N}$ = Supply Time-of-Use Rate, in ¢/kWh rounded to the thousandths of a cent, is the computed charge corresponding to each STOU Period (P), where peak is the off-peak, intermediate, or on-peak, Voltage (V), where voltage is Secondary, Primary, or High Voltage, and the corresponding Summer or Non-Summer (S or N) season.
- $ESTOUR_{P,V,S\text{ or }N}$ = The Energy Time-of Use, which represents the energy rate that shall be multiplied by a Customers usage based upon the applicable STOU Period, voltage, and season of energy consumption.
- $CSTOUR_{P,S\text{ or }N}$ = The Capacity Time-of-Use, which represents a rate in ¢/kWh meant to collect the cost of capacity charges of Customers on the rate based upon Customers' Peak Load Contribution (PLC) and Capacity Costs.
- $TSC_{STOU}$ = Transmission Service Charge for supply time-of use Customers, which represents a rate in ¢/kWh meant to collect the cost of Transmission Service charges of Customers on the rate as described in Rider TS applied to all kWh on the on-peak block for the effective month.
- $EP_{P,S\text{ or }N}$ = The Estimated Price represents the averages of the forecasted day-ahead market for the relevant season and STOU Period based upon the best available forward-looking information available leading up to the informational filings prior to the Summer and Non-Summer periods. The Estimated Price will also include applicable Revenue Sufficiency Guarantee, Revenue Neutrality Uplift, ancillary and other market settlement costs assessed by MISO.

### 3rd Revised Sheet No. 29.003 (Canceling 2nd Revised Sheet No. 29.003)

**RIDER STOU – SUPPLY TIME-OF-USE** (variable definitions continued)

- $DLA_V$ [source: "DLAV"] = The Distribution Loss Adjustment represents a factor calculated from the Distribution Loss Factors to articulate differences in cost serving customers at different voltage levels.
