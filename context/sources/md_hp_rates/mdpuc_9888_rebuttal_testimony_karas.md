## Summary of Issues and Major Conclusions

- The Commission should accept BGE's proposed two-step revenue allocation, which uses results from the Company's 2024 electric cost of service study ("ECOSS") and moves customer classes' relative rates of return ("RROR") closer to the system average.
  - Staff's proposed four step electric revenue allocation process should be rejected as it is overly elaborate and achieves substantially similar results as the Company's two step revenue allocation.
  - The Commission should balance any reduction in Schedule T revenue with the overall revenue requirement to ensure that other customers are not disproportionately affected.
- The Commission should approve the Company's proposed Customer Charge increases for Schedules R and G. The Company's proposal moves Customer Charges closer to those supported by the Company's ECOSS and aligns similarly situated customers in Schedules R/RL and Schedules G/GS.
