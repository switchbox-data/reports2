# Docket 25-55: Attachment DOER-1-2(a), Heat Pump Rate Design with Summer Rates Held at R-1/R-2

Attachment DOER-1-2(a), a workbook in Eversource's 6/11/25 response to DOER's first information requests in D.P.U. 25-55. DOER-1-2 asked whether the rate could be revenue neutral without changing summer distribution and transmission charges, and for an updated ES-HP-2 showing it; this workbook holds summer at R-1/R-2 levels and solves winter. We keep it as the design the DPU later ordered.
