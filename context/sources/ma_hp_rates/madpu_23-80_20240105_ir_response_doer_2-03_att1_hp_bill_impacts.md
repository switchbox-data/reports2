# Docket 23-80: DOER 2-03 Attachment 1, Heat Pump Bill Impacts

Attachment 1 to Unitil's Jan 5, 2024 response to DOER 2-03, which asked for electric and gas bill impacts by consumption decile for gas-to-electric heating converts under HP-RES and HP-RES LI. Four tabs show RD-1 and RD-2 electric bills and R-3 and R-4 gas bills, using the Exhibit Unitil-JDT-6 usage increment. Keep it for how a utility estimated adopter bill impacts from a design proxy rather than metered load.

## Sheets

- **P1. RD-1 to HP-RES** (86 rows x 26 columns): DPU 23-80
- **P2. RD-2 to HP-RES** (88 rows x 33 columns): DPU 23-80
- **P3. R3 with HP-RES** (34 rows x 13 columns): DPU 23-80
- **P4. R4 with HP-RES** (39 rows x 15 columns): DPU 23-80
